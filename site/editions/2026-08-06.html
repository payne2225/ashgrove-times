Customs refunds $100B in tariffs struck down by Supreme Court
About 60% of the money collected under the voided emergency-powers duties has been cleared.
By Wire Reports

U.S. Customs and Border Protection has refunded about $100 billion in import duties that the Supreme Court struck down in February, roughly 60% of the $165 billion collected on more than 53 million shipments.

The duties were imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act, which the court held in a 6-3 decision does not confer the power to set tariffs. More than 330,000 importers have filed about $127 billion in claims, and about $29 billion is still under review.
