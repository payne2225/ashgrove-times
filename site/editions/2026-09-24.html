Trump greets Xi on the tarmac as U.S. and China extend trade truce
The Chinese leader's first state visit in 11 years brings a White House arrival ceremony and a state dinner Thursday
By Wire Reports

Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland outside Washington on Wednesday evening for a three-day state visit, and President Donald Trump met him on the tarmac with a red carpet, a 21-member honor guard and a B-1 bomber flyover, ABC News and Al Jazeera reported.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the two governments agreed Wednesday to extend the trade truce reached in Busan, which had been due to lapse Nov. 10, through Jan. 10. The extension keeps tariff cuts in place and holds off new trade restrictions while the leaders meet again in China in November and in Florida in December, ABC News reported.

Xi and Trump are scheduled for a White House arrival ceremony, a review of troops and a black-tie dinner with technology executives Thursday, with a National Archives tour Friday. Trade, artificial intelligence, the Iran war and Taiwan are on the agenda, and NPR reported the two sides are still negotiating an extension of a separate rare earths truce.
