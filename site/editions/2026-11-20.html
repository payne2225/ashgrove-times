Anthropic closes 17% above IPO price as Dow sheds 1,101 points
The $81.3B offering is the largest ever; the Dow's 49,097 close was its first below 50,000 since June
By Wire Reports

Anthropic shares closed at $97.17 on the Nasdaq Thursday, 17% above their $83 offer price, after the largest initial public offering in history raised $81.3 billion, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1,101 points, or 2.2%, to 49,097 as more than 90% of S&P 500 stocks declined.

The stock, trading as ANT, opened at $111.50 near noon ET, 34% above the offer price, and touched $114.99, valuing the company above $2 trillion, before paring gains through the afternoon, CBS News reported. The S&P 500 fell 2% and the Nasdaq 2.3%; the Dow's close was its first below 50,000 since June, CNBC reported. Shares slipped more than 2.5% after hours, NBC News reported.

Analysts cited no single catalyst and pointed to doubts about returns on AI spending; Vital Knowledge's Adam Crisafulli wrote that "people just don't have much enthusiasm for this market." On Friday the Nikkei 225 fell 5.1%, the Kospi 5.2% and the Taiex 6.2%, Al Jazeera reported. Anthropic's filing showed a $104 billion net loss for the first nine months of 2026.
