Trump declares Russia-Ukraine energy ceasefire; Kyiv reports new strikes
Zelensky says Ukraine learned of the deal from the media; Moscow has not confirmed it
By Wire Reports

President Donald Trump said Sunday that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an immediate "energy ceasefire" in the 4 1/2-year war, offering no details, and within hours Ukrainian officials reported a Russian strike on a power plant in Kharkiv, followed by another on an energy facility outside the city early Monday.

Trump's post on Truth Social said only that the ceasefire was in effect, adding "Do not break it!" Moscow did not comment. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky told the Warsaw Security Forum on Monday that Kyiv had not been consulted and learned of the deal from the media, and that it would halt strikes on Russian energy sites if Russia did the same.

Two European officials told CNN the State Department and Pentagon warned over the weekend that the U.S. could suspend intelligence sharing and allied purchases of U.S. weapons for Ukraine if the refinery strikes continue. It followed Trump's deal Friday to buy Russian diesel, 300,000 tons at once and 500,000 in November, and his call Saturday for Ukraine to replace Zelensky.
