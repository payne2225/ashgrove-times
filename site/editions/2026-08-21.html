The yield on the 30-year Treasury bond reached 5.3% this week, its highest since 2007, PBS NewsHour reported, as heavy government and corporate borrowing drove up the return investors demand for lending to the government.

David Lynch of The Washington Post told the program that companies have borrowed about $500 billion this year for artificial-intelligence infrastructure, competing with governments for scarce capital. The national debt passed $40 trillion this week. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Wednesday he would increase buybacks of long-dated debt; stocks fell again Thursday as that relief faded.
