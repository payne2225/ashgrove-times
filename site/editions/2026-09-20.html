White House bars CNN, MS NOW and Politico reporters as outlets vow fight
Secret Service disabled three correspondents' passes Saturday; press groups call the ban unconstitutional
By Wire Reports

The White House barred journalists from CNN, MS NOW and Politico on Saturday, a day after President Donald Trump announced the ban, and Secret Service officers confiscated or disabled their press credentials at the gates, the outlets said. Trump announced the move Friday, citing what he called the outlets' constant reporting of "FAKE NEWS."

MS NOW correspondent Akayla Gardner said an officer asked for her badge around 8 a.m. and told her it was disabled, CBS News reported. CNN's Betsy Klein and Politico's Cheyenne Haslett were also turned away. MS NOW said it would take "any and all steps necessary" to defend its First Amendment rights, and Politico said it would "vigorously defend" its journalists.

White House Correspondents' Association President Jacqui Heinrich said courts have already ruled the administration violated the Constitution by pulling press passes over coverage. Jameel Jaffer of the Knight First Amendment Institute called the ban "pretty clearly unconstitutional." A separate case over Trump's 2025 ban on The Associated Press is still pending.
