Hurricane Lowell lashes Kauai with 84 mph gusts on its overnight pass
The Category 2 storm passed near Niihau early Tuesday with 110 mph winds; Kuhio Highway is closed at Hanalei and schools are shut on Kauai and Oahu
By Wire Reports

Hurricane Lowell passed near or just west of Niihau overnight as a Category 2 storm, the Central Pacific Hurricane Center said in its 11 p.m. HST advisory, which put the center about 90 miles west of Lihue with sustained winds of 110 mph, moving north-northeast at 18 mph as hurricane conditions spread across Kauai County.

The Honolulu Star-Advertiser reported gusts of 84 mph at Barking Sands and 79 mph at Lihue Airport. The Hawaii Emergency Management Agency said several thousand customers on Kauai and Oahu had lost power. Kuhio Highway stayed closed at the Hanalei Bridge, a flash flood warning for Kauai ran to 1:30 a.m., and Kauai officials sounded the Niihau siren as a final signal to move indoors.

Forecasters expect 4 to 8 more inches of rain on Kauai, storm surge of 2 to 4 feet and surf of 20 to 30 feet before Lowell weakens to a 70 mph tropical storm late Tuesday and moves away to the northwest. Public schools, county and state offices and courts on Kauai and Oahu are closed Tuesday. No injuries had been reported by late Monday.
