Russian missiles kill 6, cut power to 600,000 despite Trump ceasefire claim
Zelensky says Kyiv got no warning from partners; adviser says Starlink limits disrupted drone missions
By Wire Reports

Russia fired 39 ballistic missiles and more than 120 drones at Kyiv and Kharkiv on Tuesday night and early Wednesday, killing at least six people and knocking out power to about 600,000, Ukrainian officials said, three days after President Donald Trump announced an "energy ceasefire" between the two countries.

The Russian Defense Ministry said it struck two power and heating plants in Kyiv, a heating plant in Kharkiv and a substation. President Volodymyr Zelensky said Kyiv's CHP-4 plant was hit, 26 electricity facilities and four oil and gas sites were damaged, and Ukraine detected the missiles only as they neared the capital, with "no early warning from our partners."

Zelensky adviser Serhiy Beskrestnov said SpaceX limited military Starlink terminals moving faster than 160 kph without notice, disrupting several drone missions; SpaceX did not comment. In Washington, 12 senators, six from each party, wrote Trump on Tuesday urging him to drop his Russian diesel deal, calling it "a betrayal of our Allies."
