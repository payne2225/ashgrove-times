Magnitude 7.7 quake off Flores kills at least 20 in Indonesia
Landslides cut the island's main highway; a tsunami warning was lifted after no sea-level change.
By Wire Reports

Amagnitude 7.7 earthquake struck off the Indonesian island of Flores at 5:58 a.m. local time Saturday, killing at least 20 people and injuring six, the head of the Maumere search and rescue agency, Fathur Rahman, said. The U.S. Geological Survey put the epicenter 68 kilometers north-northwest of Ende at a depth of 10 kilometers.

Eight of the dead were recovered from a landslide at Reok village, and landslides in Ende regency cut the Trans-Flores highway, the 700-kilometer road running the length of the island. About 2,000 villagers in Nagekeo regency left for shelters. Indonesia's BMKG agency issued a tsunami warning and lifted it after no significant sea-level change; three aftershocks followed within 30 minutes.
