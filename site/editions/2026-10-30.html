Binance says hackers stole $6.3B in largest crypto theft on record
The exchange halted withdrawals and said about $3B tied to the attackers is frozen; analysts point to North Korea
By Wire Reports

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, said Thursday that hackers stole about $6.3 billion in what would be the largest crypto theft on record, and it suspended deposits and withdrawals while it works with law enforcement to freeze funds tied to the attackers.

Binance said the attack began with an engineer's compromised laptop, which let the attacker reach a service container and submit unauthorized withdrawals. The company said about $3 billion tied to the hackers had been identified and frozen and that it would cover all losses. Tether and Circle said they froze billions in assets linked to the attackers, NBC News reported.

Blockchain intelligence firm TRM Labs said it assessed with high confidence that North Korea's TraderTraitor unit, linked to the Lazarus Group, carried out the attack, Al Jazeera reported. The previous record was the $1.5 billion Bybit theft in February 2025. Bitcoin fell about 5% after the news, dropping below $77,000, Al Jazeera said. Binance said it would publish a post-mortem.
