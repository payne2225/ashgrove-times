Private jet nose-dives onto Palm Beach County road, killing all 9 aboard
Pilot radioed a mayday as the Learjet fell 20,000 feet a minute; 600 evacuated from a mobile home park
By Wire Reports

ALearjet 60 carrying nine people nose-dived onto a roadway in a residential area of Palm Beach County, Florida, about 12:50 p.m. Sunday, killing everyone on board, Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said. The jet had left Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas bound for North Eleuthera in the Bahamas, the FAA said.

Air traffic control recordings carried by LiveATC.net captured the pilot reporting light, clear ice and moderate turbulence at 35,000 feet and asking to climb 2,000 feet, then a mayday as the jet descended at 10,000 and then 20,000 feet per minute. "We're going to crash into the ground," the pilot said. Gov. Ron DeSantis said no one on the ground was reported hurt.

Palm Beach County evacuated about 600 residents of a mobile home park near the wreckage, County Commissioner Joel Flores said, and the sheriff's office closed Military Trail in both directions. The NTSB is investigating with FAA support. The jet is registered to a Delaware limited liability company, FAA records show; those aboard were not identified.
