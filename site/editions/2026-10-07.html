Russian missiles and drones kill at least 11 across Ukraine overnight
A five-story apartment block in Pryluky was destroyed; Zelensky calls the strike a birthday 'gift' to Putin
By Wire Reports

Russia fired dozens of missiles and 130 drones at Ukrainian cities overnight into Wednesday, killing at least 11 people and destroying a five-story apartment building in Pryluky, in the northern Chernihiv region, Ukrainian officials said, in what President Volodymyr Zelensky called one of the biggest attacks since the full-scale invasion began in 2022.

Ukraine's air force said Russia launched 48 cruise missiles, an unspecified number of ballistic missiles and 130 drones, and that air defenses downed 161 of them. Interior Minister Ihor Vyhivskyi said nine people, including three children, were killed in Pryluky; Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said two died in a fire in the city's Darnytskyi district.

Russia's Defense Ministry said it carried out a "massive strike" on military-industrial sites in Kyiv and other regions, including plants making Flamingo cruise missiles, and on Odesa port facilities; it did not mention civilian casualties. The attack fell on Putin's 74th birthday and followed drone strikes Tuesday on two cargo ships off Bulgaria, one of which sank.
