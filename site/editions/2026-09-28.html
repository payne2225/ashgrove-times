Trump says Iran talks resume this week, won't rule out new strikes
Eight Marines were hurt in a Sept. 14 missile strike on their ship in the strait, NBC News reported
By Wire Reports

President Donald Trump said Sunday he expects U.S. talks with Iran to resume this week, a day after he rejected Tehran's seven-day plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, and he declined to rule out further strikes on Iran before the November midterm elections, telling Fox News that strikes were "possible."

"They want to make a deal, but it is not the deal that I want to make," Trump told Axios by phone. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said mediators had not yet conveyed the U.S. position and Iran stands "ready for diplomacy." NBC News reported, citing three U.S. officials, that eight Marines had smoke inhalation and possible concussions after an Iranian cruise missile hit their ship Sept. 14.

The war began Feb. 28. British police arrested five men early Sunday near RAF Fairford, the Gloucestershire base U.S. bombers have flown from, on suspicion of preparing a terrorist act, and about 85 households were evacuated. Trump said "we caught them"; British Defense Secretary Wes Streeting said Monday he was not drawing a connection with Iran.
