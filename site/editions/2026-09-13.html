Ship strike kills 1 in Hormuz as Houthis fire on Saudi base
Qeshm's governor blames a 'terrorist enemy' a day before Gulf ministers meet Iran in Oman on the strait
By Wire Reports

An Iranian cargo ship was struck off Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz early Sunday, killing one crew member, Iranian authorities said, hours after Yemen's Houthis announced a missile and drone attack on a base in southern Saudi Arabia.

Qeshm's governor, Amir Teymouri, said the projectile had not been identified and blamed a "terrorist enemy," Euronews reported. The U.K. Maritime Trade Operations monitor said a vessel in the strait was hit by an unknown projectile about 5 a.m. local time, caught fire and was being evacuated. Outlets differed on how many crew were wounded, and Iran did not say who fired.

Saudi civil defense said a Houthi projectile wounded two people and damaged a mosque in Jazan's al-Tuwal governorate on Saturday, Al Jazeera reported; Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said the group hit weapons depots at the Sharurah base in Najran but did not say when. Iran hosts Gulf foreign ministers in Oman on Monday on Hormuz shipping. Bahrain said it will not attend.
