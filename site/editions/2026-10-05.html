Flavio Bolsonaro tops Lula in Brazil's first round, forcing runoff
Senator takes 47.03% to the president's 45.16%, defying polls that had Lula ahead; the second round is Oct. 25
By Wire Reports

Brazil's presidential election is headed to an Oct. 25 runoff between President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro after the right-wing senator finished first in Sunday's first round and neither candidate reached the majority of valid votes needed to win outright, the Superior Electoral Court said Sunday night.

With 99.99% of ballots tallied, Bolsonaro had 47.03% of valid votes, or 56,103,033, to Lula's 45.16%, or 53,870,724, according to court figures reported by Agencia Brasil, a gap of about 2.2 million votes. Most final polls had Lula finishing first. Ronaldo Caiado and Renan Santos, with about 5.3 million votes between them, have said they will not endorse either finalist.

Lula, 80, is seeking a fourth, nonconsecutive term. Bolsonaro, 45, is the eldest son of former President Jair Bolsonaro, who is serving a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup after losing to Lula in 2022 by 1.8 points. Lula called the result "unexpected" and said a new campaign starts Monday; Bolsonaro said the vote showed "Brazil wants change."
