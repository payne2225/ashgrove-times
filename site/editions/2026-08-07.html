Trump signs orders narrowing birthright citizenship
Two immigration actions follow the Supreme Court's 6-3 rejection of his first attempt in June.
By Wire Reports

President Donald Trump signed two executive orders Thursday narrowing who qualifies for birthright citizenship and restricting visas for visitors who come to the United States to give birth.

The citizenship order is narrower than the one the Supreme Court struck down. It excludes children born to people the administration designates as alien enemies, members of foreign terrorist organizations and agents of foreign governments, along with children whose parents obtained status by fraud, NBC News reported.

The court rejected Trump's earlier order 6-3 in June, upholding a broad reading of the 14th Amendment. Trump said he believed the new actions would be constitutional; the American Civil Liberties Union said it expects them to fail in court.
