Trump says 'one or two' Iran targets remain as Tehran floats Hormuz plan
Khamenei warns of no geographic limits if war resumes; Qatar says an LNG carrier was hit Friday in the Gulf
By Wire Reports

President Donald Trump said Saturday the United States still has to "take care of" one or two targets in Iran, including a suspected underground nuclear site, as Iran's foreign minister said a proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days was under discussion with Qatari mediators and an Iranian delegation held talks in Doha.

Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei warned Saturday there would be no geographical limits on Iranian attacks if the war resumed, Iranian state media said. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said no decision had been made on new strikes and that talks "have actually never stopped." U.S. Central Command said its blockade has redirected 144 vessels.

The U.S. and Israel began striking Iran Feb. 28; a ceasefire was reached in April, and a June 18 memorandum on Hormuz navigation stalled. Qatar Gas Transport said the LNG carrier Al Mayeda was hit by "unidentified objects" Friday with no injuries. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Iran sees no need to meet U.S. officials until its conditions, including oil waivers, are met.
