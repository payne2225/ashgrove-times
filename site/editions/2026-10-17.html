Trump waives new Russia sanctions law two days before it took effect
Waiver halts 100% tariffs on the top buyers of Russian oil and gas; Zelenskyy calls it "unfortunate"
By Wire Reports

President Donald Trump on Friday waived key parts of the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia Act, the bipartisan sanctions law he signed last month, two days before its 100% tariffs on the world's five largest importers of Russian crude oil and natural gas were set to take effect Sunday.

Trump told Congress the waiver is in the national interest, as the law allows, and also halted its 500% tariffs on Russian goods and certain other sanctions, CBS News reported. The law, drafted by Graham and Sen. Richard Blumenthal and passed by wide margins in both chambers, mainly targeted China, India and Turkey.

The pause came a week after Trump eased sanctions on Russian diesel and announced a deal with Vladimir Putin to import it; a bipartisan group of senators called those waivers a "betrayal of the people of Ukraine." Putin envoy Kirill Dmitriev called Friday's decision historic. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called it "unfortunate."
