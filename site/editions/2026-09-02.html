U.S. widens strikes on Iran; Tehran answers at bases in three countries
Iran says one strike hit a wedding near the Strait of Hormuz, killing at least 5; Jordan intercepted 10 missiles.
By Wire Reports

The U.S. military struck Iranian air-defense sites, radar, maritime assets and communications facilities Tuesday, and Iran answered overnight with ballistic missiles and drones aimed at U.S. bases in Jordan, Bahrain and Kuwait. Jordan's military said it intercepted 10 missiles, with three falling in unpopulated areas; Kuwait and Bahrain reported downing drones.

Central Command said the strikes followed attempted Revolutionary Guard attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, where two tankers were hit Monday. Iranian officials said one strike hit a home hosting a wedding in coastal Kuhestak, killing at least five people and wounding more than 60; Al Jazeera verified the location, and Central Command said it was aware of the reports.

Trump warned that Iran "will be hit again at a much harder and higher level" if it retaliates, while President Masoud Pezeshkian offered a return to the June ceasefire if Washington reciprocates. The Revolutionary Guard claimed it targeted a U.S. base near Aqaba; the U.S. said no bases were hit, and a parliament official said the strikes "will not go unpunished."
