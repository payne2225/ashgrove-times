Trump sends Witkoff and Kushner to Moscow with a plan to end the war
Zelenskyy halts strikes on Russia during the envoys' visit and asks Moscow to do the same before their Sunday stop in Kyiv.
By Wire Reports

U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner travel to Moscow on Saturday and to Kyiv on Sunday carrying what President Donald Trump called a proposal to end the war in Ukraine, Euronews and CBS News reported. "We have an idea for peace," Trump told reporters Friday, declining to say whether ceding territory is part of it. It is the pair's first trip to Kyiv since Trump returned to office.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukraine will carry out no air strikes on Russia while the envoys are in Moscow and asked Russia to do the same. Kremlin aide Yury Ushakov said this week that no long-term settlement can be expected "without solving the territorial issue," Al Jazeera reported, while Vladimir Putin said a chance for peace exists.

The trip follows a Russian drone strike Friday on the SBU security service headquarters in central Kyiv, the first on the building since the 2022 invasion, CBS reported; Al Jazeera put the injured at 12. Overnight strikes on Kamianske in the Dnipropetrovsk region killed four, Euronews reported. Witkoff and Kushner met Putin for five hours in December with no breakthrough.
