Trump promises a $5,000 'dividend' if Republicans hold Congress
At Andrews he said the Iran war 'is going to end immediately after the election'; Brent closed above $100
By Wire Reports

President Donald Trump told the Republican midterm convention in Dallas on Wednesday night that every adult citizen would get a $5,000 payment, which he called the 'Trump dividend,' if the party keeps the House and Senate on Nov. 3. 'If the Republicans win, you win with us and you get $5,000,' he said in a speech that ran an hour and 44 minutes.

He suggested tariffs could fund part of it and gave no detail on whether he would ask Congress to authorize the payments; CBS News put the cost above $1 trillion across about 245 million adults, and NPR noted federal law bars paying people to influence votes. Earlier, at Joint Base Andrews, he said of the war with Iran, 'I think the war is going to end immediately after the election.'

Brent crude closed above $100 a barrel Wednesday, at $100.19, its highest since July 24, after Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the U.S. would keep targeting Iranian tankers; the S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq each fell. Trump closes the convention tonight, sharing the final hour with Vice President JD Vance.
