House votes 211-204 to bury Hegseth impeachment for this Congress
Massie was the only Republican to vote no, 11 Democrats were absent and Jeffries called it 'just the beginning'
By Wire Reports

The House voted 211-204 Tuesday to refer Rep. Thomas Massie's eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, a procedural move that derails the floor vote Massie had forced for Wednesday and ends the effort for the eight weeks left in this Congress.

The articles accused Hegseth of waging the Iran war without congressional authorization, of the Caribbean boat strikes and of retaliating against Sen. Mark Kelly. Massie, who retires in January, was the only Republican to vote no; 11 Democrats and seven Republicans were absent, several citing a Washington traffic delay or a veterans event. Hegseth called the effort a "farce" and said he would "do it again tomorrow."

Democrats take both chambers Jan. 3, and Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries said "this is not the end of it. It's just the beginning," promising to consult members on another attempt. Massie said he will not file again but will force a war powers vote. Only two Cabinet secretaries have ever been impeached, and removal takes two-thirds of the Senate.
