Iran, Oman agree on Hormuz route as US presses for deal
The waterway, shut since late February, normally carries a fifth of the world’s traded oil and gas.
By Wire Reports

Iran and Oman have agreed on coordinates for a commercial shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz, Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmail Baghaei said Wednesday. A joint statement covering technical, legal, security and environmental terms is under final review.

The strait, which carried about a fifth of the world’s traded oil and gas, has been effectively closed since Iran shut it after U.S. and Israeli strikes in late February. Under the proposal, ships would enter the Gulf through an Iranian-controlled lane and exit through an Omani one.

Tehran wants service fees for security and environmental protection. Washington refuses. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said talks had progressed but had not reached finality. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said a deal could come within a day or two.
