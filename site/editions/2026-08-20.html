Russian missile barrage kills at least 12 in Kyiv
A nine-story block collapsed in Solomianskyi district and a children's hospital was damaged.
By Wire Reports

At least 12 people were killed and 33 wounded in a Russian ballistic missile barrage on Kyiv overnight, city officials said. Al Jazeera put the toll at 13.

Strikes hit the Sviatoshynskyi, Solomianskyi and Shevchenkivskyi districts. A nine-story residential building collapsed and caught fire and people were trapped at a second block, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said on Telegram. Two fuel facilities were struck and parts of two districts lost power.

Kyiv has few interceptors left for ballistic threats, and Al Jazeera said the shortage will tell when Russia turns to energy infrastructure before winter. Parliament confirmed Yevhenii Khmara as defense minister the day before, 312-2.
