Iran expects U.S. answer today as talks resume through Qatar
Trump posted "I offered them NOTHING!" after Axios reported sanctions relief was on the table
By Wire Reports

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said late Monday he expects a formal U.S. response Tuesday to Tehran's proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, after Qatari mediators carried messages between the two sides in New York, state media reported.

Qatari intermediaries "presented ideas, and we discussed them," Araghchi said. Trump posted "I offered them NOTHING!" after Axios reported he would grant sanctions relief and access to frozen funds for nuclear progress. A U.S. official told CBS News the talks were "positive and constructive" but no deal would come without the nuclear issue addressed.

Chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said Tuesday that if Iran's security is not guaranteed, "no infrastructure will remain safe" in the region. The war began Feb. 28. Al Jazeera reported 19 tankers transited the strait in the week ending Sept. 27 and regional exports reached 12.8 million barrels a day, the most since the war began.
