Flores quake toll climbs to 51 as thousands leave home
Indonesia counts 341 aftershocks two days after a magnitude 7.7 struck off the island
By Wire Reports

At least 51 people are dead and about 5,000 displaced after a magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck off Flores island in eastern Indonesia early Saturday, Al Jazeera reported, citing the national disaster agency. The toll has more than doubled since Saturday morning.

The quake hit at 5:58 a.m. local time about 68 kilometers north-northwest of Ende at a depth of 10 kilometers, the U.S. Geological Survey said. Thirty-six people were seriously hurt and 77 slightly. It flattened 157 houses and damaged some 1,300 more.

More than 3,500 military and police have deployed and the government is weighing a state of emergency. A quake in the same waters in 1992 killed more than 2,500 people.
