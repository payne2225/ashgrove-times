Tech sell-off erases $1 trillion as Intel posts its worst day
Nasdaq 100 falls 3.4%, its worst day since June, as investors question returns on AI spending
By Wire Reports

Technology stocks plunged Friday, erasing more than $1 trillion in market value, as Intel suffered its steepest one-day drop in decades and investors questioned whether hundreds of billions of dollars in artificial intelligence data center spending will pay off.

The Nasdaq 100 fell 3.4% and the S&P 500 lost 1.8%. CBS News put the Dow's loss at 572 points, or 1.1%. Intel fell about 29% after forecasting lower fourth-quarter sales and profit; NBC News called it the stock's worst day in records dating to the 1980s, while CBS News called it the biggest drop since 1974. Nvidia fell 4.2%, and AMD, Qualcomm and Marvell fell about 10%.

The selling followed Thursday's earnings from Amazon and Intel, a day after Alphabet shed about $500 billion in value. Amazon CEO Andy Jassy said the company still expects about $220 billion in 2026 capital spending and more in 2027. Bitcoin fell toward $77,000 after the $6.3 billion Binance theft, CBS News reported. The S&P 500 closed a third straight losing week.
