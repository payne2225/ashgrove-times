Treasury launches 'Operation Economic Outcast' against Iran
Nearly 60 entities and vessels sanctioned; Trump to press foreign leaders to cut Tehran off
By Wire Reports

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on Monday unveiled the campaign the administration had billed as an economic D-Day, naming it Operation Economic Outcast and sanctioning nearly 60 entities, individuals and vessels tied to Iranian oil smuggling, nuclear and missile procurement and cyber operations.

Secondary sanctions expand into digital assets, technology, gold, aviation and shipping, and Treasury suspended licenses that had allowed certain payments to Iran. Bessent said a major financial institution doing business with Tehran will be sanctioned by week's end. "Our objective is to sever every economic lifeline that sustains this tyrannical regime," he said.

Trump is to call foreign leaders personally to press them to drop specific Iranian trade; China buys about 90% of Iran's oil exports. Iran's security chief vowed a "seismic" response, while Pakistan said talks in Tehran on ending the war made significant progress.
