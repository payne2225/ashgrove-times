Gunman kills 3 children and a teacher at Bend, Oregon, elementary school
A 16-year-old shot through the locked doors of Silver Rail Elementary; a bystander followed and killed him, police said
By Wire Reports

A16-year-old with a long gun fired his way through the locked doors of Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, shortly before 1 p.m. Thursday and killed three children, ages 5, 8 and 11, and a teacher before a community member who followed him inside shot him dead, Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz said.

A 7-year-old student and a 54-year-old teacher were in critical condition at St. Charles Medical Center, police said. The first 911 call came from a driver who said someone with a long gun had fired on their vehicle; dozens of calls followed reporting a shooter inside the K-5 school of more than 400 students. Officers arrived within three minutes and found him dead.

Police did not name the suspect, a Bend resident, or say whether he had any link to the school; the ATF received trace results on the weapon Thursday evening, NBC News reported. Gov. Tina Kotek said the bystander "heroically intervened to stop further loss of life." Bend-La Pine Schools canceled Friday classes, and a vigil was held at Drake Park.
