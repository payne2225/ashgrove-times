Shanghai takes its heaviest rain in 150 years as Dolphin moves inland
More than a million people were moved out ahead of the storm, which came ashore in Zhejiang late Sunday.
By Wire Reports

Tropical Storm Dolphin pushed inland across eastern China on Monday, a day after coming ashore as a typhoon near Yuhuan, in Zhejiang province, with sustained winds of 151 kph. A central Shanghai weather station measured nearly 313 mm of rain in the 24 hours to 7 a.m. Monday, the city's heaviest 24-hour total in more than 150 years of records.

More than a million people were moved out of the storm's path, among them more than 900,000 in Wenzhou and 215,600 in Shanghai. Zhejiang averaged 173 mm from Friday evening to Monday morning, more than earlier typhoons including Lekima and Bavi. Forecasters expect the rain to reach Anhui, Jiangsu, Henan and Hubei through Wednesday.
