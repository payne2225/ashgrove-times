Nor'easter kills one, cuts power to 100,000 as it stalls off New Jersey
New York parks crews logged 391 downed-tree calls by 9 p.m.; hundreds of Boston flights were canceled
By Wire Reports

Aslow-moving nor'easter killed a New York City Housing Authority worker in Brooklyn, cut power to about 100,000 customers across the Northeast and flooded streets and homes along the Jersey Shore on Saturday, and forecasters said the storm would turn back toward the New Jersey coast overnight and could stall there through Monday.

Leighton Brown, 56, died after a tree fell on him in Brooklyn, Mayor Zohran Mamdani said; four others were hospitalized when a second tree fell. Nine adults and a dog were rescued from flooded motel rooms in Egg Harbor Township, N.J., CBS reported. Surf City, N.J., recorded a 74 mph gust, and Ocean County's 5.7-foot tide was the third highest on record, ABC reported.

The National Weather Service said the highest tides were likely north of Atlantic City from Saturday night into Sunday, and CBS reported the storm may stall or meander off New Jersey through Monday before departing Tuesday. FEMA said its regional coordination centers were at an enhanced posture. Hundreds of flights at Boston Logan were canceled.
