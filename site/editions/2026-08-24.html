Wildfire on Reno's edge forces tens of thousands to evacuate
The human-caused Hawk Fire burned 15,000 acres with no containment; the National Guard is mobilized
By Wire Reports

Awind-driven wildfire burning into the Sierra Nevada foothills on Reno's northwest edge forced about 42,000 people from their homes by Sunday evening, with 45,000 more told to be ready to leave. The Hawk Fire had burned more than 15,000 acres since Saturday and was 0% contained.

The fire was human-caused, said Shane Akerson, a Truckee Meadows fire battalion chief. At least six people were hurt, three of them first responders. Gov. Joe Lombardo declared an emergency in Washoe County and sent National Guard helicopter crews and 60 troops.

It is the third major fire to hit northern Nevada in a month, after the Bug and Stallion fires together burned more than 130,000 acres. Nationally, 7.7 million acres have burned this year, the most at this point in a decade.
