Trump hosts Xi at White House state dinner as China promises pandas
Xi says the two leaders 'reached common understanding on many issues'; Archives tour and departure set for Friday
By Wire Reports

President Donald Trump hosted Chinese President Xi Jinping for a state dinner in the East Room of the White House on Thursday night, capping a day that opened with a Rose Garden arrival ceremony and flyover and included a roughly 90-minute closed-door meeting between the two leaders, ABC News reported.

Xi told the dinner that he and Trump had "reached common understanding on many issues" but named no agreements, CBS News reported. Earlier Thursday, Xi said two giant pandas will move to Zoo Atlanta within days and said both nations have a duty to keep AI "always under human control." Trump gave Xi a bald eagle statue, NBC News reported.

Guests included Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, Sundar Pichai, Jensen Huang and Sam Altman, ABC News reported. Xi and his wife, Peng Liyuan, are scheduled to join the Trumps for tea and a tour of the National Archives on Friday before Xi leaves the U.S. later in the day. The two countries extended their tariff truce to Jan. 10 on Wednesday.
