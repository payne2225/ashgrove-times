French police arrest nearly 1,000 as 410,000 march against Macron
The ministry counts 110,000 in Paris, where masked protesters clashed with riot police; the CGT union counts far more
By Wire Reports

French police arrested nearly 1,000 people on Saturday as the Interior Ministry counted 410,000 marchers in 289 protests across the country, and masked demonstrators threw objects and fireworks at riot police at the end of the Paris march, which authorities had moved to the city's southeastern edge. Police answered with tear gas and water cannons.

The ministry put the Paris crowd at 110,000 and the national total below the 550,000 it counted the previous weekend; the CGT union, which co-organized some marches, counted nearly 800,000 nationwide and 250,000 in Paris, AP reported. Interior Minister Laurent Nunez said the arrest figure would rise significantly. Some 70,000 officers were deployed.

The movement began in September over staffing at a high school southeast of Paris and spread into protests over fuel and living costs. President Emmanuel Macron met student representatives Friday and promised an overhaul of university admissions. Student leaders said they would keep marching; Al Jazeera reported another protest is called for next Saturday.
