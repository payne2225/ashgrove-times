Trump tells U.N. he could 'annihilate' Iran as envoys open talks
Witkoff and Kushner held about three hours of mediated talks Tuesday; Iran's president arrived to speak Wednesday
By Wire Reports

President Trump told the U.N. General Assembly on Tuesday that he must decide whether to make a deal with Iran or 'annihilate the Islamic Republic,' hours before his envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held about three hours of mediated talks with Iranian negotiators on the sidelines of the assembly in New York.

'Do I drive them into hell with no chance of survival?' Trump said, adding that he expected a deal 'right after the election.' Witkoff said the negotiators 'completed a round of discussions that we hope will prove constructive,' with Qatar and Pakistan mediating, Al Jazeera reported. Iran's conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz include an end to the naval blockade and freeing frozen funds, CBS News reported.

Iran's armed forces said the threat showed 'strategic weakness' and warned of 'more severe, crushing, and unpredictable blows,' Al Jazeera reported. President Masoud Pezeshkian arrived in New York late Tuesday to address the assembly Wednesday, the highest-ranking Iranian official on U.S. soil since the war began Feb. 28. Trump said he expects a second round of talks 'in the very near future.'
