U.S. destroys five Iranian tankers; Iran fires missiles at Jordan base
Jordan says it intercepted 18 of 20 ballistic missiles; Brent crude touched $99 a barrel
By Wire Reports

U.S. forces destroyed five Iranian crude tankers in the Gulf of Oman and near Kharg Island on Tuesday, and Iran's Revolutionary Guard answered overnight with ballistic missiles aimed at the Al Azraq air base in Jordan and two U.S. Navy destroyers, U.S. Central Command and Iranian state media said.

CENTCOM said the strikes on the Kaviz, Charminar, Horizon 1, Riesco and Derya came after the Guard fired missiles at a Navy warship twice in 48 hours; the ship evaded both, and crews were told to abandon the tankers before they were hit. Jordan's armed forces said they engaged 20 missiles and destroyed 18, with two falling in unpopulated areas and no casualties reported.

The Guard said its missiles caused "significant damage" to the destroyers; a U.S. official told NBC News the response was ineffective. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Iran will "lose tankers" each time it fires on U.S. ships. Brent crude touched $99 a barrel Tuesday and Al Jazeera reported it at $99.49 early Wednesday, six months into the war.
