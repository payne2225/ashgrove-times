Nepal warns of new flooding as border disaster toll passes 750
Disaster authority cites rising water upstream; 734 dead and 2,498 missing in Nepal alone
By Wire Reports

Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority warned Sunday of possible new flooding along the Bhotekoshi River after Chinese authorities reported increased water flow upstream, five days after a glacier collapse sent a wall of water and debris through villages on both sides of the Nepal-China border.

The combined toll passed 750 on Sunday, Euronews reported: 734 dead and 2,498 missing in Nepal, with 16 dead and 546 missing on the Chinese side. The barrier lake that had threatened a second deluge largely drained through the weekend, and rescue teams from India and China joined the search after a brief halt when it overflowed.

Large concrete and suspension bridges are gone, power lines are down, and the Prithivi Highway into Kathmandu is only partially open. Residents described repeated runs to higher ground as warnings came and went; officials said the toll is likely to rise as crews reach areas still cut off.
