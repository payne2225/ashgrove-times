S&P 500 and Dow enter correction as Nasdaq erases its 2026 gains
The Nasdaq lost 1,261 points, or 5.3%, its second-worst day of the year; the S&P 500 fell for a seventh straight session
By Wire Reports

Technology stocks drove Wall Street lower for a second day Friday, pushing the S&P 500 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average more than 10% below their recent highs and wiping out the Nasdaq Composite's gains for the year, a day after Anthropic's record initial public offering.

The Nasdaq fell 1,261 points, or 5.3%, to close at 22,618, its second-worst day of 2026; the S&P 500 lost 3.7% and the Dow 1.9%, CBS News reported. The S&P 500's seventh straight loss is its longest streak since February 2020. Anthropic fell 9.3% to $88.14, still above its $83 offer price, and Nvidia fell for the ninth time in 10 sessions.

Chipmakers led the losses on concern that data center construction will slow and that chip export rules will tighten, CBS News reported; NBC News cited a Reuters report that regulators are examining accounting irregularities at Nvidia. The Federal Reserve is expected to raise rates in December, and Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee said Thursday the evidence does not suggest a path back to 2% inflation.
