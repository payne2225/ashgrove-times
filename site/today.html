Federal agents search Rep. Ilhan Omar's home in criminal probe
Omar calls the search political intimidation; Trump says he had "nothing to do with it"
By Wire Reports

Homeland Security Investigations agents searched Rep. Ilhan Omar's Washington-area home Monday under a court-authorized warrant, the Justice Department said, in what a person familiar with the case told NBC News is a yearslong criminal investigation involving immigration and fraud.

The department's statement did not name the Minnesota Democrat or say what the investigation concerns, and no charges have been filed. Omar wrote on X that the search was "an attempt to target political enemies and weaponize federal law enforcement" and said, "I will not be silenced." Trump told reporters he had "nothing to do with it" and that investigators were "looking at her very carefully."

The search came 22 days before the midterm elections, in which Omar is running for re-election. House Democratic leaders Hakeem Jeffries, Katherine Clark and Pete Aguilar called it "deeply disturbing." Vice President JD Vance has accused Omar of immigration fraud, which she denies, and Trump raised her Somali origin again at a rally in Youngstown, Ohio, Monday night.
