Trump, Xi and Putin meet for 90 minutes in Shenzhen, issue no statement
Xi called nuclear weapons a "sword of Damocles" in opening remarks; Trump flew home as soon as the talks ended
By Wire Reports

President Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin met behind closed doors for about 90 minutes Thursday at Kylin Villa in Shenzhen, China, on the sidelines of the APEC summit, and the leaders of the three largest nuclear powers parted without a joint or separate statement.

In opening remarks carried by China's Xinhua news agency, Xi called nuclear weapons a "sword of Damocles hanging over humanity" and said they should be "completely banned and completely destroyed." Trump said the three countries are "uniquely positioned" to discuss them, and Putin said the war in Ukraine would be his focus. No readout followed, CBS News and NBC News reported.

Trump met Putin for about 40 minutes and Xi for about an hour beforehand, NBC News reported; Kremlin aide Yury Ushakov said Ukraine was the main topic with Putin, who left "in an excellent mood," TASS reported. Russia and China have shown little interest in new arms limits; China holds about 600 warheads by Pentagon estimates. Trump boarded Air Force One at about 5:38 a.m. ET.
