Ukrainian sea drones sink Russian corvette Merkury at Libyan port
Zelensky confirmed the Benghazi strike Monday; Libya's eastern government asked the UN to investigate where the drones came from
By Wire Reports

Ukrainian naval drones sank the Russian navy corvette Merkury at its berth in the Benghazi Naval Base in eastern Libya on Sunday, President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed Monday morning, calling it "an exemplary Ukrainian operation." Satellite images showed the ship capsized beside the pier with only its bow above water and smoke over the harbor.

Seven Ukrainian-made drones were launched from a mother ship, Ukraine's SBU security service said, and Zelensky said a Ka-27 helicopter was destroyed with the corvette and the Russian cargo ship MV Angara was also hit. Libyan military officials told The Associated Press that two drones struck the Merkury and at least four were intercepted; a second drone hit a commercial ship off the port that evening.

The eastern Libyan government said the Merkury, commissioned in 2023, had stopped for fuel and supplies on its way home under a cooperation agreement, and opened a joint investigation with Moscow. Prime Minister Osama Hamad asked the UN Security Council to examine whether the drones came from western Libya, where officials have said Ukrainian specialists operate. The Kremlin referred questions to the military.
