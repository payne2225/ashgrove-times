Explosion and fire at Alberta military base kill soldiers, injure dozens
Global News puts the toll at 7 dead citing military sources; Edmonton doctor says hospitals are overwhelmed
By Wire Reports

An explosion and fire during training at Canadian Forces Base Wainwright in east-central Alberta killed and injured Canadian soldiers Saturday afternoon, Defense Minister David McGuinty said, and Global News reported at least seven dead and dozens hurt, citing multiple military sources.

Canadian Armed Forces sources told Global News a live-fire exercise struck a gas line or gas well about 4 p.m. local time, and the fire crossed the base fence in winds gusting to 100 km/h. Dr. Warren Thirsk, an Edmonton emergency physician, told CBC News hospitals near the base were "completely overwhelmed" and patients were being sent 180 km to the city.

The military said Sunday morning it would not confirm casualties until next of kin are notified; McGuinty said he could not recall an incident like it. The RCMP is investigating, Saskatchewan is sending help, and the municipal district said the fire was being held by 11 p.m. Elections Alberta closed two advance polls early before Monday's independence referendum.
