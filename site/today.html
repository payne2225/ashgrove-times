Russia frees jailed American teacher as Witkoff meets Putin
Stephen Hubbard, held since 2022 on mercenary charges, was at a St. Petersburg airport, Putin said
By Wire Reports

Russia freed Stephen Hubbard, a retired Michigan schoolteacher jailed on mercenary charges, as U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff met President Vladimir Putin in St. Petersburg on Wednesday, Witkoff and Russian state media said. Putin told Witkoff that Hubbard was at the city's Pulkovo airport, the TASS news agency reported.

Hubbard was detained in Ukraine in 2022 and sentenced after a closed trial in 2024 to nearly seven years in prison. Prosecutors said he signed a contract with the Ukrainian military; he denied it, and the State Department designated him wrongfully detained. President Donald Trump said Tuesday he had pressed Putin on the case: "I've asked him to release him."

The meeting followed talks Tuesday in Oslo between Witkoff and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy that a U.S. official said produced "meaningful progress." Witkoff is to carry Ukrainian and European proposals to Putin, who still demands all of Ukraine's Donetsk region. ABC News, citing U.S. officials, reported a deal to free more jailed Americans was near but not final.
