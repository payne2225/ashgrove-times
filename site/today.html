Donalds and Jolly win Florida primaries for governor
Moody and Nixon take the Senate nominations as DeSantis reaches his term limit
By Wire Reports

Rep. Byron Donalds won Florida's Republican primary for governor Tuesday and former Rep. David Jolly won the Democratic nomination, setting the November race to succeed Gov. Ron DeSantis, who is term-limited.

Donalds, who had President Donald Trump's endorsement, took 47.8% against 25.2% for Jay Collins and 10.5% for James Fishback, with 99% counted, NBC News reported. Jolly, a former Republican, won 61% to 15.1% for Dayna Foster.

Ashley Moody took the Republican Senate nomination and Angie Nixon the Democratic, beating Alex Vindman. The Cook Political Report rates the governor's race Solid R.
