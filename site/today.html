Canada sets dollar-for-dollar tariffs on U.S. goods for Sept. 8
Carney says Washington 'asked too much and offered too little' after 50% U.S. duties took effect
By Wire Reports

Prime Minister Mark Carney said Saturday that Canada will put retaliatory tariffs on American steel, dairy, appliances, farm equipment, paper and electronics on Sept. 8, after 50% U.S. tariffs on about $20 billion in Canadian goods took effect at midnight.

Canada will match the duties dollar for dollar, Carney said in Ottawa: 'We take this step reluctantly.' U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said Canada declined to finalize a deal reached earlier in the week and that no new talks are planned.

Carney said late changes to the U.S. terms 'called into question the reliability of any deal'; Trump posted that Canada 'wants the benefits of being a State, without being one.'
