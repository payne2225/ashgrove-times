Riyadh airport attack kills 12; Trump weighs joining war on Houthis
Saudi aviation authority says 309 wounded and an American among the dead in the deadliest Gulf strike of the Iran war
By Wire Reports

Amissile strike on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Saturday killed 12 people and wounded more than 300, Saudi Arabia's General Authority of Civil Aviation said early Sunday, the deadliest attack on a Gulf Arab state since the Iran war began. The Saudi-led coalition in Yemen blamed the Houthis and promised a response.

The dead were four Saudis, two Bangladeshis, a Palestinian and one citizen each of the United States, Egypt, Jordan, Syria and Sudan, the authority said, with 309 wounded. The missile hit the complex housing domestic terminals 3 and 4, a regional official told The Associated Press. Earlier Houthi-claimed strikes on the airport this week killed three Saudis.

President Donald Trump said he was evaluating whether the U.S. should join Saudi strikes on the Houthis. "We may. We're going to look at it," he said, and said he would call Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. Secretary of State Marco Rubio confirmed the American death. The Houthis had not claimed the strike, a week into a Saudi-led offensive against them.
