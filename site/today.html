Nasdaq falls into correction as AI selloff wipes out midterm rally
The Dow sheds 1,833 points and Nvidia drops 9.2% as investors weigh Democrats' scrutiny of AI data centers
By Wire Reports

Wall Street posted its worst day since April 2025 on Thursday as investors dumped chip and artificial intelligence stocks, sending the Dow Jones Industrial Average down 1,833 points, or 3.4%, and the Nasdaq Composite into a correction a day after Democrats' midterm sweep had produced the Dow's biggest gain of the year.

The Nasdaq fell 1,422 points, or 5.5%, to 24,315, more than 10% below its October record, and the S&P 500 lost 335 points, or 4.4%, to 7,354, CBS News reported. NBC News reported the S&P 500 shed $2.8 trillion in value and the Philadelphia semiconductor index fell 12.3%, its worst day since March 2020. Nvidia fell 9.2% and AMD 17%.

Analysts tied the rout to fears that a Democratic Congress and newly elected state officials, Texas Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa among them, will slow data center construction; Joe Maher of Capital Economics told CBS News the midterms could derail the AI boom. The selling spread Friday, when South Korea's Kospi fell more than 8% and trading was briefly halted, NBC News reported.
