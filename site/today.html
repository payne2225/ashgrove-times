SpaceX catches a Starship returning from orbit for the first time
Tower arms grabbed Ship 42 at Starbase on Saturday, 22 hours after liftoff; Friday's booster was lost over the Gulf
By Wire Reports

SpaceX's launch tower caught a Starship upper stage descending from orbit at Starbase, Texas, at 4:20 p.m. ET Saturday, the first recovery of the vehicle's ship from space. The catch came about 22 hours and 14 orbits after Friday's 5:47 p.m. liftoff, the company said.

Ship 42 reached an orbit about 165 miles up, released 26 Starlink V3 satellites and came down belly-first through low cloud before relighting three of its six Raptor engines, flipping vertical and settling into the tower's arms, CBS News reported. SpaceX said the heat shield appeared healthy. The company had caught three boosters before Saturday but never a ship.

The Super Heavy booster was not recovered: SpaceX said an automated abort during coast sent it to a Gulf splashdown after its boostback burn ended early on low fuel, and it broke apart, Euronews reported. NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman called the catch "almost magic"; NASA is counting on a Starship variant for the Artemis Moon landing it targets for 2028.
