Section of the Crimean Bridge collapses after a second night of Ukrainian strikes
Russia suspended road and rail traffic across the Kerch Strait; missiles hit two Kyiv bridges the same night
By Wire Reports

Asection of the 19-kilometer Crimean Bridge collapsed overnight after Ukraine struck the road and rail link between Russia and occupied Crimea for a second straight day, and Russia's Transport Ministry confirmed Tuesday that traffic across the Kerch Strait is suspended, the Kyiv Independent and Al Jazeera reported. Video showed one steel arch and the roadway between two piers gone.

Ukraine's SBU security service said one arch and the automobile span beneath it were "completely destroyed." President Volodymyr Zelensky called the strike the seventh phase of a weeks-long operation that first took out air defenses, radars and electronic-warfare systems, crediting Neptune missiles, Palianytsia, Ruta and FP-5 drones and a Flamingo cruise missile. The ministry said preliminarily there were no casualties.

The Kremlin named Deputy Prime Minister Marat Khusnullin to lead a commission and opened a terrorism investigation; aviation expert Andrii Kharuk estimated the fallen section at 6,000 tonnes and a year or more to rebuild. The same night, Russian ballistic missiles hit Kyiv's Northern and Southern bridges over the Dnipro, which Mayor Vitali Klitschko said are closed; emergency services reported two deaths in Obolonskyi district.
