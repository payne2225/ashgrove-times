🌊 Tides: their water — the Hampstead sound gauge — runs a midday high at 2:21 PM (3.9 ft), then falls all afternoon and evening to an 8:53 PM low (1.1 ft); that long fall is the red-drum window, and today it runs well past dark. The ocean/inlet reference (Ocean City Beach pier) leads out front: high 1:23 PM, low 7:49 PM. The sound lags the inlet about an hour — a touch more on the ebb (~65 min) than the flood (~60), since it drains slower than it fills, so that evening window stretches even longer than the clock says.

🌙 Moon: waxing crescent, 37% lit and climbing toward first quarter — so these are gentle neap tides (today's ocean swing just 3.4 ft), the inlet current soft. Easy water, not the funnel it runs on a full moon.

🎣 What's running: (monthly report, published Aug 3 — 16 days old, not a live read.) August is red-drum prime: topwater plugs at first light, then cut menhaden or finger mullet as it heats up — and the reds want the last of the falling tide, so that long afternoon-into-evening ebb toward the 8:53 low is your window (bring a headlamp, it runs past dark). Speckled trout are a dawn game in the heat — topwater in the first hour after sunrise, paddle tails in the deeper holes later. Surf City Pier's giving up specks, pompano, sea mullet, spots and the odd red drum, plus Spanish over 3 lbs on plugs; Jolly Roger's the trophy end — tarpon to an estimated 110 lbs and a 63-lb cobia on live king rigs off the end. Flounder are thick but stay catch-and-release; the harvest window doesn't open till September.
