🌦️ Friday, August 21

The week-long soaker finally lands — on two mountain towns, and skips everybody else

Owning it up front: I chased a big Friday soaker all week — two to four inches, "the river gets a good soaking too." It didn't pan out. The rain pulled itself into a narrow mountain band — Summersville and Cowen catch real showers today — while the whole river crew from Parkersburg to Huntington stays flat dry, and Hinton, the seat I'd billed at three inches, barely gets a sprinkle. The broader rain chance slid to the weekend (below).

📍 Huntington — Trav

Bone dry and drying out. Rain's a no-show (1–4%), skies mostly bright, and the swamp air keeps backing off — afternoon humidity down around 57–60% at Parkersburg and Apple Grove, and Huntington the driest, hottest seat of all at just 44%. Highs: Parkersburg 82°F (28°C), Apple Grove 84°F (29°C), Huntington the warm end near 85°F (29°C); the muggy edge is gone and lows tonight are comfortable — Parkersburg 63°F (17°C), Apple Grove and Huntington 65–66°F (18–19°C). UV pushes 7 under that bright sky, so a little SPF on the long afternoon. So much for the soaker down here — it's porch weather, not poncho weather, right through the 8:14 sunset.

📍 Summersville — Clayton

The one seat that actually caught it. The mountain showers I promised the whole crew set up right here — a 34% shot, but locally heavy, and the models stack up as much as a couple inches if a slow storm parks over the plateau this afternoon. Coolest and muggiest going: high only 79°F (26°C) down to 61°F (16°C), afternoon humidity a heavy 77%. Keep anything you don't want soaked under a roof — this is the corner where the week's forecast finally came true.

Banks the good air, too: Saturday's another sunny 28°C before showers move in Sunday.

🏖️ Topsail Beach, NC — beach & inlet report

🌊 Tides: their water — the Hampstead sound gauge — runs an afternoon high at 3:56 PM (3.9 ft), then a long fall all evening to a 10:34 PM low (1.2 ft): that's the red-drum window, and today it runs well past dark. The ocean/inlet reference (Ocean City Beach pier) leads out front: high 3:23 PM, low 10:00 PM. The sound lags the inlet about 40 minutes on the high and ~53 on the low — it drains slower than it fills, so that evening ebb stretches longer than the clock says.

🌙 Moon: just past first quarter, 58% lit and building toward gibbous — so the tides are easing off the neap, gentle but firming (today's ocean swing 3.1 ft). Not the full-moon funnel yet, but the current's got a little more push than it did midweek.

🎣 What's running: (monthly report, published Aug 3 — 18 days old, not a live read.) August is red-drum prime: the guides all say the same — get out early, topwater plugs at first light, then switch to cut menhaden or finger mullet as the sun climbs. The reds want the last of the falling tide, so that long afternoon-into-night ebb toward the 10:34 low is the window (headlamp weather, it runs past dark). Speckled trout are a dawn game in the heat. Surf City Pier's giving up specks, pompano, sea mullet, spots and the odd red drum, plus Spanish over 3 lbs on plugs; Jolly Roger's the trophy end — tarpon to an estimated 110 lbs and a 63-lb cobia on live king rigs off the end. Flounder stay catch-and-release; the harvest window doesn't open till September.

🗓️ Weekend outlook

The rain I kept chasing doesn't vanish — it just runs the weekend instead. Saturday's the iffy one: scattered afternoon storms fire crew-wide, river towns around 50% (Parkersburg 52, Hurricane 56, Apple Grove 47, Huntington 39), the mountains higher at 75–80%. Not a washout, but cookout plans want a morning window or a tarp on standby — highs mid-80s river, upper-70s mountains. Sunday dries out on the river (30–35%, cooler highs low-80s) while Summersville and Cowen stay showery, then Monday clears to a crisp, dry one — lows sliding into the low-to-mid 50s. Up at The Hart: sunny Saturday (28°C) before showers Sunday. And Vermont flips — Wes stays dry through Saturday, then Sunday turns wet (80%+).
