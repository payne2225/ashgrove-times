The front lands — Ohio's leftovers this morning, the real line this afternoon, then the first 62° night in weeks
By Jim Claudtore

Advisory's over; the front showed up early to gloat. Slight Risk (2/5) of severe storms, damaging wind, from Huntington, Lesage and Hurricane up over Summersville, Cowen and Hinton; Marginal (1/5) at Parkersburg and Apple Grove. Friday's grade: the afternoon round was real, I put it on the wrong seat — Parkersburg got the 2:35 watch and kept its shirt; warnings fired at Lesage, Hurricane, Summersville and Cowen at 4:35. Overnight quiet, as promised. Two acts: a decaying Ohio storm complex crosses the river through mid-morning, then the front's own line rebuilds on it — noon to 5 PM downriver, 3 to 5 at Apple Grove. Highs 82–88°F (28–31°C), humidity 62–71%, tonight 62–67°F (17–19°C). Sunday and Labor Day hold dry, 81–87°F (27–31°C).
