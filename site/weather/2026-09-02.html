The advisory files for overtime — extended through Thursday 8 PM, index to 104
By Jim Claudtore

The Heat Advisory didn't end tonight after all — the National Weather Service extended it through 8 PM Thursday, heat index up to 104°F (40°C), same Ohio River seats from Parkersburg down through Huntington and Lesage. I sold Wednesday as the peak; the crown moved to Thursday instead — which also picks up a cold front and a Slight Risk (2/5) of severe storms on the Parkersburg–Apple Grove end, Marginal (1/5) downriver. The wave doesn't fade, it gets escorted out. Today: dry, sunny, 93–98°F (34–37°C), and the feels-like runs 100–104°F (38–40°C) from noon to 5.

Huntington — Trav

The advisory's worst number lives at your place: 98°F (37°C) actual, and the 3 PM feels-like hits 104°F (40°C) — the full advertised index, delivered to the door. Afternoon humidity 35%, rain 1%, tonight a nearly useless 74°F (23°C). Midday errands are the ones to cancel.

Parkersburg — Pat · Apple Grove — Nate & Ian

Same advisory, one notch down: Parkersburg 93°F (34°C) on 49% afternoon humidity, Apple Grove 95°F (35°C) at 39% — both feel 101–102°F (38–39°C) mid-afternoon, both only ease to 73°F (23°C) tonight. Rain chance is a flat zero.

  • Pat: 93 makes Parkersburg the advisory's shallow end. Still deep enough to drown in.
  • Nate: the porch stays low-90s until well past the 7:57 sunset — a lager out there tonight holds regulation temperature for about four minutes. Plan accordingly.
  • Ian: Logan comes to Ashton tomorrow at 6:00, and the early read is the wave's hottest day with the front's storms possible by evening — full kickoff-hour call in tomorrow's briefing, but walk in with the lightning plan already in your pocket.
Summersville — Clayton

The plateau's discount is down to pocket change: 89°F (32°C) on 57% humidity, tonight 69°F (21°C) — and Thursday prints 90°F (32°C), right as flagged Monday. Still no advisory paperwork up the hill.

The Hart, Prince George — Kirsten

Fog patches burn off to mainly sunny and 23°C (73°F), humidity 66% — but the south wind gusts to 40 km/h again, so day two of keeping the porch furniture on a leash. A 40% shot of showers slips through overnight, low 10°C (50°F) — the open-window streak rolls on. Thursday flips to steady rain and 17°C (63°F); today's sun is the one worth being out in.

Honorable mentions
  • Lesage — Justin: 97°F (36°C) / L 74°F (23°C), humidity 35% — the volunteer rain-gauge network filed another Lesage 0.00, and the coordinator has now asked Justin a third time to stop using the observation form's remarks field, which is for weather.
  • Hinton — Greg: the New River side finally prints 90°F (32°C) — hottest of its run, still no advisory down in the gorge. Humidity 50%, tonight 68°F (20°C).
  • North Bennington — Wes: 73°F (23°C) / 58°F (14°C), humidity 68% — the stalled front feeding today's severe weather across New York sits nearly on your doorstep, but Vermont's cut is gray skies and 46% showers. Thursday's wetter, 62%.
Quick hits
  • Cowen, WV: H 87°F (31°C) / L 67°F (19°C), humidity 58% — the mountains sit out the heat until Thursday's storms clip them instead.
Topsail Beach, NC — beach report

90°F (32°C) down to 76°F (24°C), afternoon humidity 77% on a 78°F dew point — the sea breeze is just the ocean exhaling on you. Rain 10%. UV 7.4 — September hasn't repealed burn math.

Confidence: high on today's heat — a dry ridge under an advisory doesn't miss. The real wobble is Thursday: whether the front's storms arrive before or after dark decides how loud the exit is (and whether a soccer match survives it).

Tonight: the night gives back almost nothing — 73–74°F (23°C) on the river — so front-load tomorrow's water tonight; Thursday is the advisory's actual peak.
