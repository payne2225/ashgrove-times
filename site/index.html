Democrats pick a progressive in Minnesota, a centrist in Wisconsin

By Wire Reports

Democratic primary voters in six states divided between the party's left and its center Tuesday, nominating Lt. Gov. Peggy Flanagan for the U.S. Senate in Minnesota while narrowly rejecting a democratic socialist for governor in Wisconsin.

Flanagan, 46, beat Rep. Angie Craig, who held the fundraising edge, running with endorsements from Sens. Bernie Sanders and Elizabeth Warren. Milwaukee County Executive David Crowley edged state Rep. Francesca Hong in Wisconsin, and Connecticut Democrats replaced 14-term Rep. John Larson with Luke Bronin.

Trump's endorsements ran uneven. Rep. Tom Tiffany took the Republican nomination for Wisconsin governor, but MyPillow founder Mike Lindell lost Minnesota's to House Speaker Lisa Demuth.
