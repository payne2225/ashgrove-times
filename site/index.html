Kushner meets Hamas chief in Cairo before Netanyahu talks
Hamas would disarm and Israeli forces withdraw. Netanyahu rejected the plan last week.
By Wire Reports

U.S. negotiator Jared Kushner met Hamas chief Khalil al-Hayya in Cairo on Sunday for more than two hours, seeking the group's commitment to a 15-point American road map for Gaza. Egyptian, Qatari and Turkish officials attended with envoy Steve Witkoff.

The plan would have Hamas hand its weapons to a Palestinian technocratic committee while Israeli forces halt attacks and withdraw, with an international force between them. Hamas said it wants Israel pulled back to the yellow line before a 14-day negotiating period opens.

Kushner, Tony Blair and Board of Peace director Nickolay Mladenov meet Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Monday. Netanyahu rejected the road map last week, saying Israel will not leave any position until Hamas is disarmed.
