Iran rejects Trump's Hormuz claim as two more tankers are hit
The UAE called the attacks piracy; 15 of the company's ships have been hit since February.
By Wire Reports

Two vessels tied to Abu Dhabi's national oil company were attacked while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on Thursday evening, hours after Iran's military dismissed President Donald Trump's claim of American control of the waterway as lies.

ADNOC said nobody was hurt. It was the second attack on the company's ships in days and the 15th since February. The UAE foreign ministry called Iran's use of the strait as economic coercion piracy and a direct threat to the region. Iran did not comment.

Trump said Wednesday the United States has total control of the strait; Iran's central military command said it is under the complete control of the Islamic Republic. Eight ships crossed Tuesday, the fewest since Aug. 5.
