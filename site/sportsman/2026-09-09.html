Around the Leagues
Kirishima wins 19 of 21 at the joint practice four days before the basho

Ozeki (champion) Kirishima, seeking promotion to yokozuna (grand champion), won 19 of 21 bouts Wednesday at the Tokitsukaze group's joint practice at Ryogoku Kokugikan, beating new sekiwake Fujinokawa seven times in eight bouts. "I have no nervousness at all," he said. On the Water

West Virginia

  • Williams River — 54.9 cfs, 1.34 feet, falling. 55 cfs - prime wading water. Down from 101 cfs at this hour yesterday and back to low, clear late-summer water. At 55 cfs this is a long-leader, small-fly river: a 12-foot leader to 6X, a size 16 or 18 caddis or a small stonefly nymph through the pocket water and plunge pools, with caddis, stoneflies and sculpin the forage, and a small olive sculpin along the undercut banks before the sun is on the water. NWS Charleston's 3:34 a.m. discussion has a cold front bringing showers and storms in this evening, widespread overnight and Thursday, with heavy downpours and training storms that could prompt localized flash flooding Thursday into Friday. The window is today.USGS 03186500
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 24.9 feet, steady. 24.9 ft — normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Flat at normal pool for a second day, and that is structure fishing: shad are the forage, so a white or pearl swimbait or a 3/4-ounce spoon over the wing-dam tips at first light, then a football jig or a Carolina-rigged craw dragged into the slack behind the tips once the sun is up. With no current to speak of, the bite is on the shade side of the riprap and the barge cuts through midday. The front due tonight puts rain on the Kanawha and Ohio tributaries Thursday and Friday, so the pool side above Byrd will colour and rise later in the week; this is the day before that.USGS 03201500
  • Ohio River at Huntington — 26.37 feet, steady. 26.4 ft — normal pool. Ordinary bank access. The Weather Service's forecast for the Huntington gauge, issued 10:10 a.m. Tuesday and read at 5 a.m., has the river at 26.0 feet by 8 a.m. today, 25.8 by early Thursday and 25.7 by Sunday, no flooding; the NWS observation at 5 a.m. was 26.45 feet, and the action stage at Huntington is 48 feet, so the river is 21 feet under it. Normal pool below Byrd means fishing structure, not current: shad and skipjack are the forage, so a skipjack-pattern swimbait or a white spinnerbait along the riprap and the lock wall early, and a crawfish-coloured jig pitched into the slack behind the barge cuts and the wing-dam tips when the sun gets on the water. Whatever current there is runs on the lock wall on lockage.USGS 03206000
