On the Water

West Virginia

  • Williams River — 53.7 cfs and falling, 1.33 feet. 54 cfs - prime wading water. Down from 80.6 a day ago and the lowest of the week - the river is a chain of pools joined by ankle-deep riffles now, and every fish in it is looking up. Stay low and fish upstream: a 12-foot leader to 6X, a single ant or beetle dropped tight under the undercut banks, and a small olive sculpin swung slow through the two or three plunge pools still holding depth. Caddis, stoneflies and sculpin are the forage here, and at this flow the terrestrials on the bank are what the trout see most.USGS 03186500
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 25.1 feet and steady. 25.1 ft - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Pool water, up a tenth in a day - the wing-dam tips and the riprap are the structure and shad the forage, so a white swimbait or a spoon over the tips at first light, then a football jig dragged down the seam once the sun is on the water.USGS 03201500
  • Ohio River at Huntington — 25.9 feet and steady. 25.9 ft - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Steady below the dam after Thursday's small rise, so the current on the lock wall and in the barge cuts is ordinary again - a crawfish-colored jig pitched into the cuts and a skipjack-pattern swimbait along the riprap where the flow pins bait against it.USGS 03206000

North Carolina

  • Topsail Sound — Sound highs 2:13 AM at 3.3 ft and 2:52 PM at 4.4 ft at Hampstead; lows 8:35 AM at 0.4 ft and 9:48 PM at 0.9 ft. Fish the moving water either side of high at 2:13 AM and 2:52 PM in the sound. The Fisherman's Post September report, published Tuesday and three days old, has the red drum bite "steady when fishing docks and creek mouths" on early topwater, flounder "going wild" at 20 to 24 inches with the mullet thick inshore, and Spanish and blues hit-or-miss on Gotchas off the Jolly Roger and Surf City piers; the shops are still waiting on the fall king bite. Flounder want moving water on structure, so work the flood into the 2:52 PM high; reds want the last of the fall, into tonight's 9:48 PM low. Last-quarter moon at 51%, so neap tides and a gentle inlet. Water 85.6F at Beaufort, 60 miles up the coast.NOAA CO-OPS 8657813
