On the Water

West Virginia

  • Williams River — 82.1 cfs and falling, 1.53 feet. 82 cfs - prime wading water. Down from 128 a day ago and the thinnest of the week - pool-and-drop pocket water now, so fish it like September: a long leader off the rod tip, one beetle or ant tight under the overhangs, and a small sculpin swung through the two or three bends still holding depth.USGS 03186500
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 25.09 feet and steady. 25.1 ft - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Pool has not moved in a day - the wing-dam tips are still the morning spot, a white swimbait over shad early, then a dragged jig on the seams when the sun gets on the water.USGS 03201500
  • Ohio River at Huntington — 25.64 feet and steady. 25.6 ft - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Steady below the dam - crawfish colors tight to the riprap and the lock wall, and a jig pitched into the barge cuts where fish hold off the current.USGS 03206000

  • Topsail Sound — Sound highs 12:22 AM at 3.5 ft and 1:01 PM at 4.1 ft at Hampstead; lows 6:43 AM at 0.4 ft and 7:42 PM at 0.9 ft. The September report is finally fresh - Fisherman's Post published its Topsail monthly yesterday: Spanish and blues on Gotchas off the piers, reds on topwater early inshore, and flounder now legal against pilings and creek mouths.
