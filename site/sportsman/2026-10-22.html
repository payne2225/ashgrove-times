Ohtani will not need surgery and is expected to pitch in 2027, Friedman says

Dodgers president of baseball operations Andrew Friedman said Wednesday that doctors prescribed rest for Shohei Ohtani's left knee and right arm and that "I fully expect him to pitch next year," though how the two-way workload is managed is undecided. Ohtani had eight wins, two losses and a 1.79 ERA in 14 starts but did not pitch after July 3, hit .294 with 30 home runs through Aug. 18, then had 11 hits and 29 strikeouts in his last 70 regular-season at-bats as Los Angeles was swept by Milwaukee in the NLCS. Angels decline Suzuki's option after the franchise's first 100-loss season

The Angels said Wednesday they declined Kurt Suzuki's 2027 option after one season as manager, in which Los Angeles went 62 wins and 100 losses, 10 fewer wins than in 2025 and the first 100-loss season in franchise history. Interim general manager John Mozeliak said he will not stay as full-time GM in 2027 and that the new GM, to be hired once Stan Kroenke takes control from Arte Moreno in the first quarter of 2027, will pick the manager. Suzuki, offered a special-assistant role, told The Orange County Register the decision "wasn't a surprise" with ownership changing. The Angels have not reached the postseason since 2014.
