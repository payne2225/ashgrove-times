On the Water

West Virginia

  • Williams River — 141 cfs and falling, 1.84 feet. 141 cfs - prime wading water. Down from 179 cfs on Friday and lower again, so the pocket water and plunge pools are the whole river. Long leader and a small caddis dry, with a light stonefly nymph through the deeper pockets and a sculpin pattern tight to the undercut banks.USGS 03186500
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 25.12 feet and steady. 25.1 ft — normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Normal pool fishes structure. Shad are the forage, so a white swimbait along the wing dams and the riprap, and a crawfish-coloured crankbait tight to the rock.USGS 03201500
  • Ohio River at Huntington — 27.4 feet and steady. 27.4 ft — up and pushing. Expect colour and current; fish the slack behind anything solid. Up and coloured, so the fish are off the main current. Work the slack behind the wing dams, the barge cuts and the lock wall rather than the open channel.USGS 03206000

North Carolina

  • Topsail Sound — Sound high 11:50 AM at Hampstead; lows 5:51 AM and 6:14 PM. Fish the moving water either side of high at 11:50 AM in the sound. Tide first: the push into the 11:50 a.m. high and the fall behind it are the windows, and it is a single high in daylight today. Mud minnows, shrimp and finger mullet are the forage, so work the grass edges and the oyster bars through the moving water.NOAA CO-OPS 8657813
