Liverpool beats Club Brugge 3-0 as Isak scores twice in the Champions League

Liverpool defeated Club Brugge 3-0 at Jan Breydel Stadium in Bruges on Wednesday before 27,286 in the Champions League league phase, per ESPN's match page. Alexander Isak scored in the 13th minute from Dominik Szoboszlai's pass and again in the 82nd from Alisson Becker's long clearance, taking his season total to 10, and Florian Wirtz curled in from outside the box in the 79th after Rio Ngumoha's break. Brugge had 17 shots to Liverpool's 13 and 53.5% of possession but put five on target, all saved. Victor Munoz went off injured in the 42nd minute. Liverpool are 5th in the 36-team table on 12 points from five matches, Brugge 13th on nine; Liverpool play at Everton on Sunday at 7 a.m. ET.

Reds bring back catcher Tyler Stephenson on a two-year deal with a player option

Onosato's seventh title closes on Terunofuji's Reiwa-era mark; Hatsu is 45 days out

Yokozuna (grand champion) Onosato's November title in Fukuoka, his second in a row and seventh overall, puts the 26-year-old a step closer to overtaking Terunofuji atop the list of championships won in the Reiwa era, which began in May 2019, the Japan Times' John Gunning wrote Wednesday: all seven of Onosato's Emperor's Cups (top-division titles) have come in the current era, against nine of Terunofuji's 10. The Japan Sumo Association's schedule page lists the banzuke (rankings) for the Hatsu basho (New Year tournament) for release Dec. 21, with the tournament opening Jan. 10 at the Kokugikan in Tokyo, 45 days out, and closing Jan. 24.

At 71 cfs and clear on Thanksgiving morning the Williams is a long-leader, small-fly problem, thinner than Wednesday's 76: the fish sit in the pocket water and the tailouts of the plunge pools, not on the seams, and the warmest hours of the day fish best.
