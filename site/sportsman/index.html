  • Williams River — 101 cfs and falling, 1.64 feet. 101 cfs - prime wading water. Down from 176 a day ago and settling into true low-summer form - thin, clear and spooky. Fine tippet and one small dry, ants and beetles under the shaded banks through midday, and the deeper bends once the sun is fully on the water.USGS 03186500
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 24.88 feet and steady. 24.9 ft - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Steady at pool - shad are the forage, so run a swimbait off the wing-dam tips at first light, then slow to a jig dragged along the same seams as the sun climbs.USGS 03201500
  • Ohio River at Huntington — 25.51 feet and steady. 25.5 ft - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Holding steady below the dam - work a crawfish crankbait tight to the riprap and lock wall, and pitch a jig into the barge cuts where fish sit off the current.USGS 03206000
