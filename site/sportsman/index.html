NFL will not punish Eagles over Hurts playing with a blood clot, reports say

The Athletic reported Tuesday, and ESPN confirmed, that the league found no violation of its injury-report policy in Philadelphia's handling of Jalen Hurts, who played the Week 5 game in London after a clot in his calf was diagnosed as superficial and is now expected to miss at least three months on blood thinners after clots were found in his lungs. Hoshoryu will return at the Kyushu basho, his stablemaster says, 18 days out

Tachinami oyakata (stablemaster), the former komusubi Asahoho, said Tuesday that yokozuna (grand champion) Hoshoryu, 27, who had surgery on his right knee in late July and missed the September tournament, his third straight absence, "will come back from Kyushu," having rejoined the autumn jungyo (exhibition tour) on Oct. 10. Hoshoryu has gone 10 tournaments as yokozuna without a yusho (championship), one short of the record since 1958. The Kyushu basho (tournament) runs Nov. 8 to 22 at the Fukuoka Kokusai Center, with the banzuke (rankings) due Monday; the Japan Sumo Association also announced a 15,000-seat spring exhibition at Tokyo's Ariake Arena on April 15.
