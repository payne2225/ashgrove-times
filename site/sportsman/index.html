Alonso says Chelsea were not bullied at Sunderland but did not produce enough

Xabi Alonso said Monday that Sunday's loss at the Stadium of Light was a situation Chelsea could have defended better rather than a case of being bullied, that the match was even until Enzo Le Fee's 36th-minute penalty and that his side did not produce enough once behind; asked about Brian Brobbey, who won the penalty off Levi Colwill and was substituted in the 67th minute, Alonso said the striker caused a few problems out of almost nothing and is stoppable with better defending. Reds first baseman Sal Stewart wins the National League Rookie of the Year award

Sal Stewart, 22, the Cincinnati Reds' first baseman, was named NL Rookie of the Year on Monday with 20 of 30 first-place votes from the Baseball Writers' Association of America, ahead of the Mets' Carson Benge (seven) and the Cardinals' JJ Wetherholt (three); Stewart hit 32 home runs with 112 RBIs, the first 30-homer, 100-RBI season by a Reds rookie, batted .263/.330/.457 with 15 stolen bases and played all 162 games, which he said was his greatest source of pride. Tigers infielder Kevin McGonigle, 22, took the AL award with 28 of 30 first-place votes after leading the league with 7.1 Baseball-Reference WAR.

Fujiryoga pushes down Hoshoryu for his first kinboshi on day 3 of the Kyushu basho

Maegashira (rank-and-file) 1 Fujiryoga, 23, in his first meeting with yokozuna (grand champion) Hoshoryu, met the yokozuna's thrusts, unbalanced him when Hoshoryu reached for a right overarm grip and could not get it, and pushed him down over the edge by oshitaoshi (push down) in the final bout of day 3 at the Fukuoka Kokusai Center, Hoshoryu's first loss since returning from three tournaments out with a right knee injury; Fujiryoga, who had lost both his previous bouts against a yokozuna, both to Onosato, and now has two wins and a loss, said attacking forward without pausing was what worked, and his kinboshi (gold star, a rank-and-filer's win over a yokozuna) came 11 tournaments and three days after his debut at makushita 60 in March 2025, the seventh-fastest for a wrestler who entered through the tsukedashi (amateur-credit) route. Japan Sumo Association chairman Hakkaku said the yokozuna's legs are not yet fully right and called it the effect of the injury.

Onosato forces down Asanoyama, but Kirishima loses to Yoshinofuji and no yokozuna or ozeki is unbeaten

Yokozuna Onosato, chasing a second straight yusho (championship), took his right inside grip against maegashira 1 Asanoyama's left overarm hold, stayed on him and turned him down by yoritaoshi (force out and down) for a second win, denying the former ozeki (champion) a first kinboshi in seven years; komusubi Yoshinofuji, day 1's conqueror of Onosato, threw ozeki Kirishima by uwatenage (overarm throw) after Kirishima went for a leg-hooking throw that NHK's Mainoumi called forceful and reckless, Kirishima's first loss, while ozeki Aonishiki forced out Ura and ozeki Kotozakura threw Fujiseiun. Mitonoumi, 33, the first wrestler from Okinawa to reach sanyaku (the titled ranks), beat sekiwake Fujinokawa with a left overarm throw for his first win as a komusubi, a third straight loss for the 21-year-old ozeki hopeful, who false-started twice, and Sponichi counted four men unbeaten after three days, sekiwake Atamifuji, who pushed out Daieisho, and Takanosho among them.
