In Season

Coming in

  • Black bear (Gun), Aug. 29 - Sept. 7 — WVDNR lists a gun bear season opening Aug. 29 in SELECTED COUNTIES only. The county list is not in the pamphlet's summary table, so no county is named here. Season limit 2, daily limit 1; bag limits vary by county and permit class and are on pamphlet pages 33-39.WVDNR

Prime

  • Red drum — Coastal waters: 18-inch minimum and 27-inch maximum total length, one per day. It is unlawful to gig, spear or gaff them.NCDMF
  • Spotted seatrout — Coastal waters: a 14- to 20-inch slot with one fish over 26 inches permitted, three per day.NCDMF
  • Sheepshead — Coastal waters: 14-inch minimum total length, five per day.NCDMF

Also

  • NC — Flounder is closed in North Carolina coastal waters and unlawful to possess. Topsail Sound is coastal water, so Marine Fisheries governs it, not the inland digest.NCDMF
  • WV — Nothing else opens within two weeks. The first general season is squirrel on Sept. 12, with a youth weekend Sept. 5-6.WVDNR
  • WV — Goose, duck, dove, woodcock and snipe dates are not in the summary table. They come from a separate WVDNR migratory bird publication issued in August, and HIP registration is required.WVDNR
On the Water

This morning's readings

  • Williams River — 179 cfs and falling, 2.00 feet. 179 cfs - prime wading water. Low and clear, so the pocket water and plunge pools are the whole river. Long leader, small caddis dry, and a light stonefly nymph through the deeper pockets; a sculpin pattern on the undercut banks.USGS 03186500
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 25.5 feet and falling. 25.5 ft and falling - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Normal pool fishes structure. Shad are the forage, so a white swimbait along the wing dams and the riprap, and a crawfish-coloured crankbait tight to the rock.USGS 03201500
  • Ohio River at Huntington — 27.9 feet and falling. 27.9 ft and falling - up and pushing. Expect colour and current; fish the slack behind anything solid. Up and coloured, so the fish are out of the current. Work the slack behind the wing dams, the barge cuts and the lock wall rather than the open channel.USGS 03206000
  • Topsail Sound — Sound highs 10:57 a.m. and 11:19 p.m.; lows 5:09 a.m. and 5:24 p.m., at Hampstead. Fish the moving water either side of high at 10:57 AM and 11:19 PM in the sound. Tide first: the water moving off the 10:57 a.m. high is the window. Mud minnows, shrimp and finger mullet are the forage, so work the grass edges and oyster bars, and the dock lights on the late high.NOAA CO-OPS 8657813
