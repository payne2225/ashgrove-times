Liverpool and Chelsea draw 1-1 at Anfield; Liverpool go through 6-5 on penalties

The Carabao Cup fourth-round meeting of two followed clubs finished level after two first-half penalties four minutes apart: Morgan Rogers converted for Chelsea in the 40th minute after Gravenberch fouled Caicedo, and Cody Gakpo converted for Liverpool in the 43rd after a Fofana handball. Liverpool won the shootout 6-5 after 16 kicks, Mamardashvili saving from Emegha and Joe Gomez scoring his first goal of any kind in 281 appearances before Fofana put the last kick over. Mike Penders started in goal for Chelsea and saved from Araujo and Barcola; Cole Palmer served his suspension; Florian Wirtz came off the bench in the 59th minute and scored his shootout kick; Lacroix went off injured at halftime. Attendance was 60,214. Liverpool host Bournemouth in the quarterfinals in the week of Dec. 14; Chelsea are out. Alonso called it 'a small setback, probably the least important competition for us,' and Iraola said 'luckily for us, we finished on the right side of the penalty shootout.'

Rookie shortstop Cooper Pratt drove in two runs, singling home Brice Turang in the second and adding a sacrifice fly in the sixth after William Contreras' RBI double; Tampa Bay's only run came on Chandler Simpson's ninth-inning double off Megill.
