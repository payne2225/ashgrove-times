Our Teams
Arsenal beat Liverpool 1-0 at Anfield, their first league win there since 2012

Kai Havertz tapped in at the far post in the 16th minute on Sunday after Alisson's loose pass to Jeremy Jacquet was seized on and worked right through Noni Madueke and Declan Rice. Liverpool had most of the ball after that but never forced David Raya into a save, and a Jurrien Timber tug on Alexander Isak went unpunished by VAR. Havertz's 46th Premier League goal makes him the competition's record German scorer. Arsenal go top on 24 points; Liverpool stay 5th on 15, nine back, and visit Fenerbahce in the Champions League on Wednesday.

Steelers run over the Browns 27-6 and take the AFC North lead

Jaylen Warren ran for 143 yards and a touchdown, Rico Dowdle and Pat Freiermuth also scored on the ground and Pittsburgh sacked Deshaun Watson eight times Sunday at Acrisure Stadium, the Steelers' 23rd straight regular-season home win over Cleveland, tying the NFL record. Watson passed for 324 yards, much of it late; Shedeur Sanders took a few snaps after Watson was shaken up in the first quarter, and the Browns fumbled on consecutive possessions. Jared Verse, who had said it was time to 'make Pittsburgh ours,' finished with one assisted tackle. Cleveland (5-3) visits New Orleans on Sunday.

At 62 cfs and clear on the second morning of November the pocket water, plunge pools and undercut banks the file records as its structure hold less water than on any morning of this 16-day recession; the wading is easy everywhere, the fish are pinned to what current is left, the heads and tongues of the plunge pools, the single deep slot in each run of pocket water and the shade under the cut banks, and at this clarity they see a wader from a long way off. This is a long-leader, small-fly problem, as it was Sunday: a 12-foot leader to 6X or 7X, a size 20 to 22 blue-winged olive or a small parachute Adams on top through the middle of the day, when the light and the air are warmest, a size 18 or 20 pheasant tail or zebra midge dropped 18 inches under it through the morning for the caddis and small stonefly nymphs the file records as forage, and a size 10 olive sculpin pattern or a small black woolly bugger swung slow and deep through the plunge pools at dusk, the one heavier fly the low water still allows and one the stocked fish take too; an egg pattern or a bright San Juan worm under a small indicator covers the holdover stocked pools without changing the rig. Drab clothing, a low approach from below at the pool tails and the first cast count for more than the pattern at this flow.USGS 03186500; WVNS
