Ura pushes out Hoshoryu for his first kinboshi in 25 tournaments; the yokozuna has lost 2 straight

Maegashira (rank-and-file) 2 Ura, 34, pushed out yokozuna (grand champion) Hoshoryu in the final bout of day 4 at the Fukuoka Kokusai Center on Wednesday, the second straight day Hoshoryu has given up a kinboshi (gold star, a rank-and-filer's win over a yokozuna) after Fujiryoga's on day 3: Hoshoryu opened with two-handed thrusts and then pulled back, and Ura, who had lost his first three bouts to Kirishima, Atamifuji and Aonishiki, followed him out for his first win. It is Ura's third career kinboshi and his first since beating Terunofuji at the 2022 Aki basho, 25 tournaments ago, after Harumafuji at Nagoya in 2017, and makes three different yokozuna he has beaten; Hoshoryu had won 10 of their 16 previous meetings. Ura said he was happy and had resolved to try his hardest; Hoshoryu, 27, back after three tournaments out following right knee surgery, has two wins and two losses, and Japan Sumo Association chairman Hakkaku had said before the bout that the yokozuna's legs were not yet fully right and that he needed to return to the mindset of day 1. Ura meets yokozuna Onosato in Thursday's final bout.
