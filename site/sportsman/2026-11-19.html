Kansas beats West Virginia 86-82 in the Players Era semifinal despite Sadler's 34

No. 11 Kansas defeated West Virginia 86-82 at Michelob Ultra Arena in Las Vegas on Wednesday night in the semifinals of the Players Era tournament, per ESPN's box score and the AP game story. West Virginia led 49-44 at the half and 68-58 with 11:15 to play before a 10-2 Kansas run, and still led 82-81 with 1:26 left when Tre White was fouled twice and made four free throws in the final 39.6 seconds. Freshman Miles Sadler scored 34 points on 11 of 23 shooting with 10 of 11 free throws and Martin Somerville hit four threes for 16 off the bench, but the Mountaineers were 6 of 29 from three and were called for 30 fouls; Kansas went 27 of 40 at the line to West Virginia's 24 of 27, with White scoring 21, Trey Kaufman-Renn 19 and Keanu Dawes 18 points and 12 rebounds. Kansas is 5-0 and meets Houston, which defeated No. 1 Florida 86-76 earlier Wednesday, in Thursday's final; West Virginia, 4-1, plays Florida in the third-place game Thursday at 2:30 p.m. ET on ESPN. The game did not count in the Big 12 standings, and the teams meet again at Allen Fieldhouse on Jan. 2.

Mexico beats Jamaica 1-0 to set up a March Nations League semifinal with the USMNT

Mexico defeated Jamaica 1-0 in Monterrey on Tuesday night in the second leg of their Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal, per ESPN, and advanced on away goals with the series level at three goals apiece after Jamaica won the first leg 3-2 at home, so the United States' semifinal opponent in late March at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, Calif., is Mexico rather than Jamaica.   • Williams River — 99.1 cfs, 1.63 feet at 5:15 a.m., rising on flow and steady on stage against 92.1 cfs and 1.59 feet in the fetcher's 24-hour comparison. 99 cfs - prime wading water. The gauge at Dyer reads 99.1 cfs and rising, 7 cfs over the fetcher's 24-hour comparison and about 2 cfs over the 97.3 Wednesday's paper carried at the same hour, with the stage up four hundredths to 1.63 feet; a nudge, not a bump, and still inside the late-autumn base flow the river has held all week. At 99 cfs and clear it is a long-leader, small-fly problem, with the fish on the pocket-water lies and the tailouts rather than the seams, and after another cold night the midday hours fish best once the sun is on the water. A size 18 to 20 midge or a small Blue-winged Olive nymph under a soft indicator on a long leader through the slower pockets and the tailouts is the pattern, a stonefly nymph as the point fly in the deeper plunge pools where the larger fish hold in cold water, and a sculpin pattern swung tight to the undercut banks in the first and last hour of light.USGS 03186500 (Williams River at Dyer); config.SPORTSMAN_WATERS
