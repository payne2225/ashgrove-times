Bengals list Burrow with an illness as Risner and Burks miss Thursday's practice

Quarterback Joe Burrow was listed with an illness on the Bengals' first injury report of the week, published Thursday ahead of Monday night's game at Washington, and the report marked him a full participant; the team's own practice account says he did not go through stretch and that the week's quarterback media session was swapped to Ja'Marr Chase, with Burrow to speak Friday or Saturday. Starting right guard Dalton Risner (knee) and backup linebacker Oren Burks (groin) did not practice and coach Zac Taylor called both day-to-day; cornerback Dax Hill was held out with an illness and defensive tackle Jonathan Allen was given a rest day. Taylor said receiver Tee Higgins, banged up in Sunday night's win over Pittsburgh, is good, and he is not ready to say whether receiver Andrei Iosivas, cornerback Jalen Davis or lineman Brian Parker II, all back at practice off injured reserve last week, will play. ESPN's game preview has Burrow completing 74.7% of his passes, second in the league with 2,795 yards and first with 27 touchdown passes, and the Bengals, seven wins and two losses with five straight wins, third in the NFL at 30.9 points a game with at least 31 in each of the last five. Washington, three wins, five losses and a tie, won 24 to 21 at the Giants last Thursday; kickoff Monday is 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN.

Arena says U.S. Soccer has no clue in its search for Pochettino's successor

Bruce Arena, the U.S. men's national team's winningest coach and now the San Jose Earthquakes' manager, said Wednesday that U.S. Soccer does not have a clue what it is doing in its search for Mauricio Pochettino's replacement, ESPN reported; Pochettino was confirmed as Manchester United's coach last Friday and left after Tuesday's two-goal win over Haiti in Cincinnati completed an eight-goal aggregate Nations League quarterfinal win. U.S. Soccer has said the search will begin with under-23 coach Steve Cherundolo overseeing a December camp and a friendly against Jamaica next month, per ESPN. Arena, who coached the national team from 1998 to 2006 and again in 2016 and 2017, opposed the Pochettino hiring in 2025 on the grounds that a national team coach should be domestic; his 81 wins are the program record, and his second spell ended with the failure to qualify for the 2018 World Cup.

  • Williams River — 97.3 cfs, 1.62 feet at 5:15 a.m., steady on flow and stage against 97.3 cfs and 1.62 feet in the fetcher's 24-hour comparison. 97 cfs - prime wading water. At 97 cfs and clear it is a long-leader, small-fly problem, with the fish on the pocket-water lies and the tailouts rather than the seams, and after another cold night the midday hours fish best once the sun is on the water. A size 18 to 20 midge or a small Blue-winged Olive nymph under a soft indicator on a long leader through the slower pockets and the tailouts is the pattern, a stonefly nymph as the point fly in the deeper plunge pools where the larger fish hold in cold water, and a sculpin pattern swung tight to the undercut banks in the first and last hour of light.USGS 03186500 (Williams River at Dyer); config.SPORTSMAN_WATERS
