Mac Allister's father says Liverpool have not opened renewal talks

Carlos Mac Allister told ESPN in an interview published Friday that there have been no contract renewal talks between Liverpool and his son, that 20 months remain on the midfielder's deal, that the sporting director and the head coach have both changed and the situation is under review, and that there may be news before the end of the year; Alexis Mac Allister, 27, said in September he had not been offered a new deal in the summer and chose to stay, has started every Premier League match under Andoni Iraola with one league goal and three in the Champions League, and his father said the family is talking only about Liverpool, the club that signed him from Brighton in 2023.

Hoshoryu forces out Takayasu to stop his run of kinboshi losses at 3 on day 6

Yokozuna (grand champion) Hoshoryu, back this month from three straight tournaments missed after right-knee surgery in July, charged hard at the tachiai (initial charge), got both arms inside and forced out maegashira (rank-and-file) 3 Takayasu on day 6 at the Fukuoka Kokusai Center on Friday to go to three wins and three losses, ending a three-bout losing streak in which he gave up kinboshi (gold stars, a rank-and-filer's win over a yokozuna) to Fujiryoga, Ura and Fujiseiun; Chunichi Sports reported a fourth straight would have been the first since Miyaginoyama in January 1931. Sports Hochi reported that withdrawal (kyujo) had been discussed and that Hoshoryu telephoned his stablemaster Tatsunami asking to keep fighting; he said afterward he would make no excuses for the losses and wanted to compete to the end, and judge Onoe called it a perfect bout.
