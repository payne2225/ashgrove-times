Crew goalkeeper Evan Bush will retire after the season

Evan Bush, an 18-year professional and 15-year MLS veteran who has started all 196 of his MLS regular-season appearances with 607 saves and 44 shutouts, said Thursday he will retire when the Crew's season ends. Acquired from Vancouver in December 2020 after nine seasons with Montreal, he won the 2021 Campeones Cup, the 2023 MLS Cup and Leagues Cup 2024 in Columbus. The Crew will honor him Saturday before the 2 p.m. ET regular-season home finale against D.C. United, with ninth place and the second wild-card spot at stake.
