The river's most recent stocking this paper has reported is still Thursday, Oct. 29, from WVNS's Oct. 29 list. WVNS's Oct. 30 list, published Friday at 5:12 p.m. and the newest on its fishing-and-hunting feed as of 5:47 a.m., names Glade Creek of New River, Brandywine Lake, Cacapon Park Lakes, Evitts Run, North Fork South Branch, Opequon Creek and both sections of Shavers Fork, not the Williams, the Cranberry or the Summersville tailwater, and no list dated Oct. 31, Nov. 1, Nov. 2 or today exists on that feed, so Thursday's fish have had five nights to spread out of the first pools below the truck stops. At 59 cfs and clear on the third morning of November the pocket water, plunge pools and undercut banks the file records as its structure hold less water than on any morning of this 17-day recession; the wading is easy everywhere, the fish are pinned to what current is left, the heads and tongues of the plunge pools, the single deep slot in each run of pocket water and the shade under the cut banks, and at this clarity they see a wader from a long way off.
