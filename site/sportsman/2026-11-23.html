Liverpool beat 10-man Manchester United 4-0 in Pochettino's first match in charge

Liverpool defeated Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield on Sunday in Mauricio Pochettino's first match as United's coach, per ESPN's match report and box score. Leny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute for denying Alexander Isak an obvious goal-scoring opportunity just outside the box; Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the resulting free kick in the 10th, Florian Wirtz hooked in Rio Ngumoha's cross in the 13th, Ngumoha scored in the 66th from Bradley Barcola's pass on a break and substitute Victor Munoz added the fourth in the 82nd from a Wirtz through ball. Liverpool had 28 shots and 11 on target to United's 10 and three, with 63.8% of possession, and Senne Lammens made seven saves. Liverpool move to fourth on 21 points from 11 matches; United are 19th on 8 and have gone nine league matches without a win, per ESPN's report. Liverpool play at Everton on Sunday at 7 a.m. ET.

Onosato sets an 80-win target for 2027 as the Hatsu basho sits 48 days out

Yokozuna (grand champion) Onosato told a press conference at his stable's Fukuoka lodgings on Monday, a day after winning the Kyushu basho (tournament) in a playoff for his second straight yusho (championship) and his seventh overall, that it was his first Kyushu title and "a very fresh feeling," the Mainichi Shimbun reported via Yahoo News Japan. He said the left shoulder he dislocated at last year's Kyushu basho, which cost him this year's spring and summer tournaments, had left him "close to rock bottom" and unable to see his own sumo, that he now thinks his sumo is complete, and that he is aiming for 80 wins in 2027 and the year's most-wins title. The Yokozuna Deliberation Council, the Japan Sumo Association's advisory body, met in Tokyo on Monday, and chairman Tadamori Oshima said members felt Onosato had fulfilled a yokozuna's responsibility and dignity and that they would rather commend yokozuna Hoshoryu for finishing nine wins and six losses after his early defeats, Nikkan Sports reported; he said the council wants both yokozuna contesting the title on the final day next year. The JSA's tournament schedule lists the Hatsu basho at the Kokugikan in Tokyo from Sunday, Jan. 10, through Jan. 24, 2027, 48 days from today, with the banzuke (rankings) released Dec. 21; the JSA's English champions interview page for Kyushu was still unposted at 5:52 a.m. ET.

  • Williams River — 83.8 cfs, 1.54 feet at 5:15 a.m., falling on flow against 92.1 cfs and steady on stage against 1.59 feet in the fetcher's 24-hour comparison. 84 cfs - prime wading water. The gauge at Dyer reads 83.8 cfs and 1.54 feet, the fetcher's 24-hour comparison 92.1 cfs and 1.59 feet, so the river is down another eight cfs on the day and under 85 for the first time in the run of readings this paper has carried; the stage trend reads steady. At 84 cfs and clear it is a long-leader, small-fly problem, the fish on the pocket-water lies and the tailouts rather than the seams, and in late-November water the midday hours fish best once the sun is on it. A size 18 to 20 midge or a small Blue-winged Olive nymph under a soft indicator on a 12-foot leader through the slower pockets and the tailouts is the pattern, a stonefly nymph as the point fly in the deeper plunge pools where the larger fish hold in cold water, and a sculpin pattern swung tight to the undercut banks in the first and last hour of light. Water this thin and this clear asks for a longer cast, a finer tippet and an approach from downstream.USGS 03186500 (Williams River at Dyer); config.SPORTSMAN_WATERS
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 24.88 feet at 5 a.m., steady against 24.64 in the fetcher's comparison. 24.9 ft — normal pool. Ordinary bank access.
