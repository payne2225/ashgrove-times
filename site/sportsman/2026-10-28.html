Chelsea visit Liverpool in the Carabao Cup at 4 p.m. ET without Palmer, Wirtz a doubt

Liverpool host Chelsea at Anfield on Wednesday in the Carabao Cup fourth round, 4 p.m. ET (8 p.m. GMT) on Paramount+, with Darren England refereeing, in the first meeting of two followed clubs this season. ESPN's team news lists Cole Palmer suspended after two yellow cards in Saturday's defeat at Tottenham, goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez out with a broken jaw, Reece James and Jamie Gittens out, and Joao Pedro a doubt with a muscle injury; Mike Penders is expected to start in goal. Around the Leagues
Rays walk off the Brewers 4-3 in the 12th to deny a sweep and force Game 5

Milwaukee was one inning from its first World Series title and lost it. Cooper Pratt's leadoff homer off Ian Seymour in the top of the 12th put the Brewers up 3-2 Tuesday night at Tropicana Field, and Shane Drohan then faced five Rays without an out: Junior Caminero reached on Joey Ortiz's throwing error, Liam Hicks and Jonny DeLuca singled, pinch-runner Cedric Mullins scoring to tie it, Chandler Simpson singled to load the bases and Ben Williamson's single to right won it. Tampa Bay out-hit Milwaukee 9-8 and the Brewers made two errors; David Hamilton's fifth-inning homer had put Milwaukee up 2-1 before 10 scoreless innings from both bullpens. Milwaukee still leads three games to one; Game 5 is Wednesday at 8 p.m. ET at the Trop, with Jacob Misiorowski starting for the Brewers.

Colts trade Nelson to Seattle and Buckner to the 49ers as a rebuild begins

Indianapolis dealt two cornerstones to the NFC West on Tuesday, two weeks before the Nov. 10 trade deadline. Eight-time Pro Bowl guard Quenton Nelson, 30, goes to the Seahawks for a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fifth-rounder; Seattle waived guard Christian Haynes to make room. Three-time Pro Bowl defensive tackle DeForest Buckner, 32, returns to San Francisco, which traded him to the Colts in 2020, for a 2027 fourth-round pick. Both are in the final year of their contracts, and the Colts have lost three straight since firing their general manager. Elsewhere Tuesday, the Bengals put tight end Erick All Jr. on injured reserve with what is believed to be a torn ACL, the Giants put running back Tyrone Tracy Jr. on IR with a hamstring injury, and Green Bay's Matt LaFleur said Micah Parsons could practice next week for the first time since knee surgery.

Mississippi State loses QB Kamario Taylor for the season to toe surgery

Kamario Taylor, the sophomore who carried Mississippi State to four straight wins to open the season, at Minnesota and South Carolina among them, needs surgery on the toe he injured against Missouri on Sept. 26 and will miss the rest of the season, the school said Tuesday; he tried to play through it against Alabama and Oklahoma and left both games early. Lincoln Riley, asked about his future at USC after a second loss left the Trojans' playoff hopes thin ahead of Ohio State on Saturday, said 'this is where I want to be' and called his relationship with athletic director Jen Cohen 'fantastic.' Iowa running back Kamari Moulton is out for the season with an upper-body injury, and Alabama dismissed defensive back Dre Kirkpatrick Jr., who faces misdemeanor charges. The first College Football Playoff rankings come out Nov. 3.

Fourth-tier Fleetwood knock Arsenal out of the Carabao Cup on penalties

Fleetwood Town of League Two put Arsenal out of the Carabao Cup on Tuesday, winning the shootout five kicks to three after a one-all draw in the round of 16, one of the biggest upsets in the competition's history. Aaron Loupalo-Bi put Fleetwood ahead in the 21st minute, Viktor Gyokeres equalised from close range in the 50th, and Arsenal finished with 10 men after Max Dowman was sent off for violent conduct in the 85th. Bradford City also advanced, past Peterborough United at Valley Parade. Wednesday's ties include Chelsea at Liverpool, Brighton at Manchester City, Aston Villa at Bournemouth, Crystal Palace at Fulham and Brentford at Sunderland. At Manchester United, Michael Carrick took training Tuesday and, per ESPN sources, expects to be in charge against Chelsea on Saturday; the club has made no announcement.
