On the Water

West Virginia

  • Williams River — 48.8 cfs and falling, 1.29 feet. 49 cfs - prime wading water. Down from 65.6 cfs a day ago and the lowest reading of the week - the river is a string of pools now, and every trout in it is looking up. Long leader to 6X, a single ant or beetle dropped tight to the undercut banks, and a small olive sculpin swung slow through the plunge pools that still hold depth. Caddis, stoneflies and sculpin are the forage here; at this flow the terrestrials falling off the bank are what the fish see most.USGS 03186500
  • Ohio River at Point Pleasant — 25.27 feet and rising. 25.3 ft and rising - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Up from 24.83 feet a day ago, so a touch more push on the pool than yesterday - the wing-dam tips and the riprap are the structure and shad the forage, so a white swimbait or a spoon over the tips at first light, then a football jig dragged down the seam once the sun is on the water.USGS 03201500
  • Ohio River at Huntington — 26.98 feet and rising. 27.0 ft and rising - normal pool. Ordinary bank access. Up from 25.81 feet a day ago, more than a foot below the dam, so there is real current on the lock wall and in the barge cuts again - a crawfish-colored jig pitched into the slack behind the cuts and a skipjack-pattern swimbait along the riprap where the flow pins bait against it.USGS 03206000

North Carolina

  • Topsail Sound — Sound highs 3:08 AM at 3.3 ft and 3:50 PM at 4.5 ft at Hampstead; lows 9:36 AM at 0.3 ft and 10:47 PM at 0.8 ft. Fish the moving water either side of high at 3:08 AM and 3:50 PM in the sound. The Fisherman's Post September report, published Tuesday and four days old, has red drum "steady when fishing docks and creek mouths" on early topwater, flounder "all over the place" with an excellent class of 20-to-24-inch fish on a bucktail and Gulp trailer, black drum and pompano on the bottom off the Surf City pier, and Spanish and blues hit-or-miss on plugs off the Jolly Roger; the shops are still waiting on the fall king bite. Coastal Angler's September note adds black drum on cut shrimp at the bridges and oyster rocks. Flounder want moving water on structure, so work the flood into the 3:50 PM high; reds want the last of the fall, into tonight's 10:47 PM low. Waning crescent at 40%, so neap tides and a gentle inlet. Water 85.1F at Beaufort, 60 miles up the coast.NOAA CO-OPS 8657813
