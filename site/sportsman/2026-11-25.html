Tottenham's Williams-Barnett, 18, signs a new long-term contract after his first start

Tottenham said Tuesday that Luca Williams-Barnett, 18, has signed a new long-term contract, per ESPN; the club did not disclose its length. He scored and set up Dominic Solanke's goal in Saturday's win over Ipswich in his first Premier League start, which made him the club's youngest league scorer since Gareth Bale in 2007, and he has five senior appearances. "This is a special moment for me and my family, and one I have worked really hard to reach. But this is only the start," Williams-Barnett said in a club statement. Spurs, 10th on 15 points, play at Sunderland on Sunday at 9:05 a.m. ET.

JSA names three juryo promotions for January and lists four retirements; Hatsu is 46 days out

The Japan Sumo Association announced Wednesday in Tokyo, after the banzuke (rankings) committee met following the November tournament, that three wrestlers will be ranked in juryo (the second division, the lowest salaried rank) for the January tournament: Oikari of Isenoumi stable, from Kyoto, who takes the new ring name after fighting as Ikarigata; Gojinyu of Takekuma stable, from Hyogo; and Mudoho of Otake stable, from Tokyo, who returns to the division, per the JSA's new-juryo page. The same day the association listed four retirements from the November tournament, all from the sandanme and jonidan ranks below the salaried divisions, on its retired-wrestler page. The JSA's English pages carried no promotion to ozeki (champion, the second-highest rank) or yokozuna (grand champion) this morning. The Hatsu basho (New Year tournament) opens Sunday, Jan. 10, at Ryogoku Kokugikan in Tokyo, 46 days from today, per the JSA schedule this paper read Monday; the full January banzuke is published later in December, and tickets go on sale Dec. 5.
