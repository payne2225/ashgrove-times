Around the Leagues
Yoshinofuji beats yokozuna Onosato on day 1 of the Kyushu basho

Komusubi (the fourth-highest rank) Yoshinofuji, the Kumamoto-born local favorite, thrust down yokozuna (grand champion) Onosato in 3.2 seconds in the final bout at the Fukuoka Kokusai Center, a result the judges upheld after a mono-ii (judges' conference) over whether Onosato's hand or Yoshinofuji's foot touched down first; it was the first bout for the new tate-gyoji (chief referee), the 40th Kimura Shonosuke, who said Yoshinofuji's foot had stayed in. Yoshinofuji has won five of six against Onosato.

He meets maegashira (rank-and-file) 1 Asanoyama on day 2, having won all five previous bouts between them.
